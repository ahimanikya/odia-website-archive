Chlorine Tablets for disinfection of drinking water in flood effected area.

Dear Sir,

We are aware that your organisation is engaged in the highly noble task of relief work in the natural disaster hit areas.

We have come to know that due to the existing situation of floods and the consequent contamination of water sources, there is an imminent danger of spreading of water bourne diseases in many part of word. These can be effectively controlled by using Chlorine Tablets.

Our company Omen Drugs Pvt. Ltd. is basically a Pharmaceutical Company and we specialize in the manufacture of Chlorine Tablets which we have developed after years of research.

For the past 15 years we have been supplying the same to Govt. as well as non Govt. institutions. These tablets are cheap as well as cost effective and easy to use and carry.

We are ready to supply from our existing stock. The specification and rates are as follows :

SPECIFICATION : Each Tablet contains 45 mg. Chlorine which is useful for disinfection of 25 litre of water.

PACKING : 100 Tablets in a jar.

RATE : Rs. 15.00 per  100  Tablets f.o.r. destination.

DELIVERY : Immediate from ready stock.

PAYMENT : By Demand Draft in favour of OMEN DRUGS PVT. LTD., payable at Bhopal.

CONTACT PERSON : Dr. R.S. GOSWAMI  (098260  58605 Mobile)

(Managing Director)

Phone :  (0755)  586057 (O), 260937 (Factory)

Fax  : (0755) 756511

E-mail : odpl@bom6.vsnl.net.in

Now, I look forward for your early response.

With regards.

(Dr. R.S. GOSWAMI)
