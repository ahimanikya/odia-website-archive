PART-II 

ABOUT PROJECT PROPOSAL 

PROJECT TITLE REHABILITATION OF FLOOD VICTIMS OF BAKUGRAM OF KOTHAKASANGA OF Nimapara Block at Puri District. 

TRAGET AFFECTED ARFA ONE Village under Kothakasanga GP of Nimapara of Puri District of Orissa. 

TARGET POPULATION Male – 318 , Female- 309 Total - 627 

BACK GROUND AND JUSTIFICATION 

Natural calamity is always causing threat to human life not only ;this but also to the cattle population & environment. The recent flood in Orissa lose the house, human life. Forest, 

Paddy, Vegetable, Public utilities connect be calculated interms of money. The misery that caused to the people. If orissa will continue for another 10 years at the minimum. 

Nimapada being one of the Block of Puri Dist in orissa has been affected by flood with a very painful effect. The situation has became worst of Nimaparda Block. The Major loss in the GP area is crop which ;in the long run will deprive. People from getting food and taking up cultivation in the next seasons. No food material available to all families. Cattle are facing a great problems for food and fodder. No seed available for next season cultivation. Water sources for drinking and bath have been polluted. People of this village suffering from diseases. 

At the time of flood we the members of National Active Youth Association have involved in immediate Relief operation. We Plan to undertake some activities as a measures of rehabilitation. 

OBJECTIVE OF THE PROJECT 

To take up activities relating to agriculture and to facilitate then for their environment. 

To upgrade the skill of the farmers in all villages for change of traditional practices for getting more products. 

To facilitate them for some cash crop cultivation. 

To restore irrigation sources. 

To check migration to other states by social mobilisation. 

To ensure involvement of women folk for economic activities. 

To protect environment through Nursery & Plantation. 

PLANNED ACTIVITIES TO BE UNDERTAKEN 

Since our objective is to involve the people in all activities which will sustain the process the 

following activities shall be undertaken within one year. 

Increase the leadership attitude of the existing of people’s committees at the village level to look after agriculture Plantation nursery raising etc. 

Farmers training on agriculture to upgrade their skill for more produces from cultivation. 

Supply of seed and fertilizer to meet the immediate need and in the long run facilitation for seed, fertilizer bank and agricultural equipment center in each village. 

Initiation for economic activities by women self help Groups. 

Promotion of vegetable & cash crop cultivation by farmers. 

Nursery Raising and Plantation in this village. 

Checking of migration to other states through awareness building. 

IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY 

The Association of NAYA with the people of Bakugram since relief operation. Nimapada is the costal Block of Puri district which is situated in the bank of Bay of Bengal the river like Danua which is crossing across the said area & riches the Bay of Bengal. These area are flood promed due to existence of river Danua which has no embankment the people of the village mostly affected by recent flood. They are lost their lively hood & food grain. Our organization decided 

this area is need place for relief works. In that reason our organisation adopt this village as our Project Village. 

The programmes being planned are to be implemented in complete association with above active people’s committees. Excepting purchase of seed, fertilizer and agricultural equipments from outside all other are to be done by the people and under the guidance of respective committees. For farmers training 50 farmers from this village shall under go a skill development training for a period of 10 days in two phases at two cultivating seasons i.e. Paddy & Vegetable for this training resources person from Govt. Deptt. & other agencies shall be invited. 

MANPOWER REQUIREMENT

Two of our volunteer engaged in relief work since 15 days in this village. But for taking up activities relating to agriculture Horticulture & economic activities for women groups two more staffs to be required. 

Those two staffs must have adequate experience in the area and shall be completely responsible for upliftment of agriculture, horticulture & economic status of women groups. 

EXPECTED OUTCOME 

In this village there shall be committee to look after the problems relating to their own 

area/interest. 

Change in cropping systems and more yield from cultivation. 

People can be able to go for vegetable and cash crop cultivation. 

Irrigation sources to continue and soil moisture will increase. 

Seed and fertilizer Bank in this village and one agriculture implement center in this village. 

Income generating activities by women in addition to their house held activities. 

Decrease in number of migration to other states and family life will sustain. 

MONITORING & EVALUATION 

Monitoring & evaluation of the Project shall be made in two ways. 

i. By a sub committee to be formed by the executive committee of NAYA at least twice in a year. 

ii. By the Programme staff & representatives of people committees in monthly meeting both ways are to be coordinated by secretary. 

At the beginning of every 6 months the NAYA would prepaire a 6 monthly action Plan and would send one copy to the funding agency. At the end of the every 6 month NAYA will submit the progress reports of each six month. To the Funding Agency.
