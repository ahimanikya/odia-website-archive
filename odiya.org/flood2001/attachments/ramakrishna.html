Dear  Sir / Madam,

APPEAL  FOR  HELP  FOR  FLOOD  VICTIMS  OF  ORISSA

As you may be aware that Orissa is reeling under severe  flood . What makes the situation most alarming is that districts like Koraput, Kalahandi, Bolangir , Sambalpur, Dhenkanal  which are normally not affected by flood  are worst hit. Large tracts of areas have been submerged under water. As usual coastal districts of Puri, Cuttack and Balasore are severely affected. Almost all the rivers are in spate. Immediate relief in shape of food , medicine , drinking water are

urgently required . It is a time for everyone to contribute according to his mite to help the victims who have lost everything .

We  at  Sri Ramakrishna Ashrama  have already started relief work in Kalahandi. Within our meager resources we have already supplied  life saving medicines  to the affected  villagers.We have already repaired a small road washed out by the flood in Kalahandi. In fact we are undertaking mobile medical units in nearby 50 villages regularly round the year. We are contemplating to set up a center of dedicated youths well trained to help in cases of natural calamities and also to help the people combat draught conditions in Kalahandi. For this we require a permanent fund. We appeal to your good self to think over the matter

and come forward with whatever help you can give at this hour of national calamity.

We will be highly obliged to anybody who can provide us with lists of other NRIs excepting Oriyas and please do also try to persuade them to contribute liberally to this occasion . You can send your donations to

Secretary,

Sri Ramakrishna Ashrama
