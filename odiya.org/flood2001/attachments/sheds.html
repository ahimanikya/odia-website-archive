Introduction

The consistent rains in the recent days with two consecutive low depressions has resulted in increase of water in all rivers and as a result of it many embankments got breached out causing floods. Atleast five lakh people across the coastal districts have been marooned by the flood water of the rising rivers in the mahandi river system, of which are in spate due to the incessant rains in Orissa and neighbouring Chattisgarh during the past 36 hours.

The biggest threat now is with the people who are either living inside the villages which are now submerged with water or have been relocated since they are in urgent need of food and sanitation support .

Banki and Jagatsinghpur is one such area which is severly affected. A total of 30,000 families are severly affected and a lot of them are women and children who are vulnerable. The decisions to open the suice gates of mahandi has come as a shock to the people as  more water will enter the villages.

Due to the high tide in Sea the water is not able to be released into the sea and thus this situation would remain for the next ten days.

Partners Operational History

SHEDS is a registered non-government agency which has been engaged in numerous social development activities in Orissa. It has been undertaking activities related to the Reduction of Infant Mortality Rate and Under 5 Mortality rate, Making Community Health Accessible and affordable and providing education and Self Employment through various Income Generation Activities. After the Super Cyclone in Orissa SHEDS played a major role in the Relief and Rehabilitation of the affected victims.  SHEDS is registered under the Foreign Contribution Regulation Act and therefore is legally registered to receive the funds from foreign countries.

SHEDS was established in the year 1987 when a group of youth in their eight grade decided to improve their locality and create awareness about various social issues such as drugs, alcoholism, sanitation with an initial contribution of Rs.75/- (approx. $.2). However it started to concentrate on more intense activities and undertakes activities that are focussed and provide a greater impact in the long run. It is the fund channeling agency as well as the nodal agency for many donor agencies like the Oxfam, National Lotteries and India Literacy Programme apart from Concern. Presently SHEDS has 80 full time staff and 132 volunteers apart from 31 part time staff.

SHEDS has an annual budget of around Rs.50,000,000 ($.1,086,957) and is also rated as one of the major consulting agencies for the Health and Education Programmes in India. It is also notable that 40% of the funds are generated from its own source of funds.

Problem Statement

The flood situation has caused a major threat to the livelihood of the people and are in need of immediate food  and sanitation support as they have lost all their food reserves . most of the people are being relocated since their houses are submerged in water and thus they are totally dependant on external support for their living.

Objectives

To respond to the immediate food and sanitation needs of Two Hundred families in Banki Block of Cuttack District and Biridi Block of Jagatsinghpur District.

Target Group and Working Area

SHEDS is targeting all the people affected by the fllod irrespective of any caste, creed or religion. The Working area would be Banki Block of Cuttack District and Biridi Block of Jagatsinghpur District .

Time Frame

Ten days 

Activities

In order to fulfill the objective SHEDS would undertake the following activities:

Provide rations of 15 kilograms of rice and 3 kilograms of Pulses for one week.

Provide three kilograms of flat rice and five hundred grams of jaggery for the first three days.

Provide water purification tablets, ORS and water cans for the families.

Provide three candles and two match boxes to each family.

Provide one Polythene to each family.

ORS will be procured from the Government.

Work plan and Time Duration

The activities are scheduled to start from the 21st of July and the first thing that would be done is providing flat rice and jaggery for the three days. This would be followed by giving them water purification tablets, ORS and water cans in the next day or two. Since SHEDS has a database of the suppliers which came out as an outcome of the preparedness lessons learnt after the cyclone therefore it would be easier for it to procure the goods immediately.

After the third day Rice and Pulse would be provided for one week. As the water level would slowly expected to reduce after two three days since the sea would accept the water once the tides get slower it is planned that SHEDS would encourage the people to atleast rebuild their houses(depends upon the situation) as they are being provided with the ration of one week.

Selection Process

SHEDS would target all the people affected by the floods irrespective of caste. Creed and religion.

Monitoring and Evaluation

Monitoring would be conducted by the SHEDS staff and the distribution would be done by the women groups. Since SHEDS has its presence in the villages with women groups therefore these women would be utilised for assisting the activities and there would be distribution lists duly signed by the beneficiaries and only the women in the family would be given the ration on behalf of the family which will make it easier since the distribution would also be done by the women.

Apart from that  all the Senior management members are being pressed into action
