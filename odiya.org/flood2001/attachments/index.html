Flood-relief attachments

Six surviving Word documents from the 2001 flood-relief folder, restored as plain HTML with their text and tables. These records show appeals, proposed projects and contemporary assessments; their presence in the backup does not establish that a project was funded or completed.

Open the saved proposal page · Open the flood-relief website

  • SHEDS flood-relief proposal

    Food, shelter and sanitation support proposed for Banki and Biridi blocks. Linked from the saved proposal page.

  • Unnayan immediate-relief proposal

    A proposal covering 24 villages, with village and budget tables. Linked from the saved proposal page.

  • Sri Ramakrishna Ashrama appeal and proposal

    An appeal dated 28 July 2001 and a short-term Kalahandi relief proposal. Linked from the saved proposal page.

  • NAYA flood rehabilitation proposal

    An organizational profile and an agricultural rehabilitation proposal for Bakugram. Linked from the saved proposal page.

  • Orissa Floods 2001 overview

    An assessment of immediate damage, rehabilitation needs and longer-term infrastructure work. Additional file in the same backup folder; no original inbound link confirmed.

  • Water-disinfection tablet supplier letter

    A saved supplier letter from Omen Drugs Pvt. Ltd., with historical specifications and prices. Additional file in the same backup folder; no original inbound link confirmed.

Reading the record

The originals contain spelling variants and conflicting dates or budget figures. These remain visible, with notes where discrepancies were found. New page titles and navigation are restoration additions. Historical payment, contact and supplier information is retained as text only.
