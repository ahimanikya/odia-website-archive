Pushpalata

Pushpalata is the first classical Indian dance company to be formed by dancers trained solely in Britain and it was founded in 1992. The dancers have studied for many years under the Odissi and Kathak artist and teacher Priya Pawar at the Academy of Indian Dance. 'Pushpalata' means a 'cluster of flowers' and the company was named after her mother.

Pushpalata offer performances in Odissi and Kathak classical dance and folk dance. Working with Indian dance drama, based on the poetry, great Indian epics and Indian spiritual writings, is their uniqueness as company. They also offer workshops in each of the dance styles, and are particularly interested to collaborate with dancers of other dance forms.

Artistic Director: Priya Pawar

Priya Pawar grew up in Lucknow in India, where she began her daily training in Kathak at the age of four under Mohanrao Kalyanpurkar. She gained her first dance degree, 'Nritya Vishard' at the age of 10. She obtained her 'Nriyta Varidhi', a masters degree in dance, after studying under Guru Sundar Prasad in Jaipur. Priya took up Odissi when already an experienced Kathak dancer by undertaking daily intensive training under Guru Hare Krishna Behera, whom she continues to spend some time with each year, developing and composing new projects, while also having trained with Guru Kelu Charan Mahapatra Throughout most of her career, Priya has taught these styles of dance, and currently teaches Odissi at the Academy of Indian Dance, at The Place Theatre, London, and Kathak at The Hounslow Civic Centre, London.

Two of the company's first projects have included ' Chandalika - The Outcaste', based on Tagore's famous dance-drama, and 'Ritu Sanghar', based on the classic by the poet Kalidasa, reviewed in last month's edition of The Muse.
