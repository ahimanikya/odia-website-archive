Directions to Hindu Temple

From South Kansas City:

1. Take I-35 North to 435 West
2. Exit at Shawnee Mission Parkway East
3. Exit at Lackman Road and Make a Left Turn going North
4. About 1/2 mile on your left you will see the entrance to the temple

 

From North Kansas City

1. Take I-29 South to 635 South
2. Take 635 South to I-35 South
3. Exit at Shawnee Mission Parkway (63rd Street)
4. Make a Right Turn going West
5. After about 4 miles, Exit at Lackman Road and Make a Right Turn going North
6. About 1/2 mile on your left you will see the entrance to the temple

Information on Kansas City Airports


Kansas City has two airports. The major airport is the Kansas City International Airport (Code MCI) which is in the North-West outskirts of the city. It is approx. 30 miles from the Hotel as well as temple. The Downtown airport is about 7 miles from the hotel and 10 miles from the temple. However, most of the airlines operate from/to Kansas City Int'l airport.
