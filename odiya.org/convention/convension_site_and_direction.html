Venue:

 The convention on 1st September will be for half-a-day starting in the afternoon and continue till past midnight. We will have it at the convention center of the Hotel itself. On 2nd September, we will have the full day convention at the Hindu Temple of Kansas City, which is approximately 3 miles from the hotel. Arrangements have been made for food during the convention, details of which will be posted later.
