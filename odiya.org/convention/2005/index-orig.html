Dear Friends and Well Wishers of oDiA samAja:

That time has arrived. We are happy to announce that oDiA samAja is all ready to hold the bandhu miLana, 2005 in Toronto.

With the short time at disposal it was a challenge to organize this. Even so we have come with a plan of fun-filled oDiA activities on this occasion.

The bandhu-miLaNa will be held on the long weekend of July 1-4, with the apex day being Saturday, July 2. Please mark your calendar and confirm your participation as soon as possible. [click to register].
