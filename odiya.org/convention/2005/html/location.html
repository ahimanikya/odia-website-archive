Location

City of Toronto lies on the shore of Lake Ontario, the easternmost of the Great Lakes. Home to more than 4 million people, the city is the key to one of North America's most vibrant regions, the Greater Toronto Area (GTA). The cultural, entertainment, and financial capital of the nation. Link to Toronto Tourism.

Toronto is a fabulous pl ace to visit in mid summer. Excellent gardens, thrilling entertainment centers, the enchanting Lake Ontario, and, of course, the 7th wonder of the world -- the Niagra falls, and the tallest free standing structure in the world.

There are many other renowned natural wonders within a few hours drive, such as the Lake on the Top of the Mountain, the Thousand Islands, the flowerpot islands. Rather than describe each of these in detail I have attached the web links at the bottom.

Toronto is famous for its variety of restaurants with flavours that reflect it multi-cultural mosaic, particularly, the Indian cuisine.

We are very lucky that the CODC is giving a repeat performance of its Nabarasa show on July 1st. CODC is a renowned dance academy run by oDiAs. In many ways the success of CODC is a testament to what oDiA samAja is striving for.

Considering these myriad possibilities we have organized formal programme for July 2. Outside these times we will have informal programmes that can be arranged once we get rolling, and also to let our visitors take in all that Ontario has to offer.
