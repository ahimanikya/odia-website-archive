Registration


Fee Structure :We have developed a very attractive (and reasonable) fee structure consisting of 3 classes:

  • Odia Samaja Members: $35 per family
  • Non-members: $50 per family ($10 less if registered before June 15)
  • Students: $20

The registration charge does not include food, accommodation, and other associated travelling expenses.  However, for the formal day of July 2, lunch and dinner is part of the activities.  Very reasonable, and actual expenses incurred will be charged.  Organizers will provide all necessary assistance for accommodation and cuisine guidance as deemed necessary for individual cases.

Registrants are kindly encouraged to join the family of Odia Samaja by simply subscribing to the society's official forum, oDiA samAja Yahoogroup, and then paying either the annual membership dues ($25) or life membership fee ($250).  Annual dues, of course, are counted as part prepayment of life membership.

Where to Send/Ask: Intending registrants may send their fees in advance by check to "Biswa R. Patnaik" and mention "BM2005" in the Memo line and mail it to the following address.
