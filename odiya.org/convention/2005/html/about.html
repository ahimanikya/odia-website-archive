About odiSA

VISION
The vision of the Society is to see oDishA as a developed state in India

MISSION
The mission of the Society is to be one Team with one dream: to collectively pursue the dream (vision) “developed oDishA”

Read dUradRuSTi August 2003 issue to know oDia Samaja history and what it stands for or click here to download PDF version of the article.

OBJECTIVE
To be a non-profit global organization dedicated to the social, cultural, educational, linguistic, literary and economic development of oDishA (Orissa) and oDiAs (Oriyas) anywhere in the world

To be an organization free from any sort of discrimination based on caste, creed, color or religious beliefs.

To foster friendship and mutual respect amongst the members in a free, dynamic and pluralistic environment.

To instill the oDiA (Oriya) self-esteem and leadership qualities among oDiAs everywhere in the world.

To reach out to the oDiAs (Oriyas) anywhere, anytime and stand by them especially at the time of need.

Engage in developing oDishA (Orissa) by facilitating strong ties with people of oDishA (Orissa) and her developmental activities leading to a better and prosperous oDishA (Orissa).
