AsantAkAli

by

Susmita Patnaik


AsantAkAlira SakALa
Kemitee Heba Kiye Jane?
Hueta GatakAlipari Madhura
abA Ajipari NihAti Gadyamaya
Emitibi Hoiparey
SabudinathAru Vinna hoi
Sabu rutunkara Ranga Nei
Gotapane Nuakari Janma Neipare

AsantAkAli SakALe Mo GharasAmnA Gachhabrukhya
Phula Pakhi, O Rastaghatare, Niti AtajAta Samasta charitra
Sabu Huyeta Badalijibe
Parichita Manisatiku Khojibaku jai
Aainare Munha Dekhu Dekhu
Keundinu HajijAithibA
abA Kebehin GhatinathibA
JaNa ajaNa Kathasabu Smruti pari Swapnapari
Mo ChAripate luchakaLi KheLuthibe

AsantAkAlira KhabaraKagajare
Premara Sangya Huyeta BadaLi jaithiba
Sabu Daihika, MAnasika ChetanAku BAd dei
GatakAlira Abhidhanare
Prema Khali Sabdatiye Hoi Rahijaithiba

Sabu Iccha Aniccha Satwe
AsantAkAli Pruthibiru AkAsajayen
Huyeta Sabukichi Badalijiba
SetebeLe Tame KaNa KhAli
Tame hoithiba? nA Ekebare Vinna MaNisate Hoi
Mo ChAripatey AtajAta hoi bi
Mate tike Itahsthata Karunathiba ?

Jadi Satakatha Emiti
Tebe Ajira rAti Barang Na pAhu
abA AsantAkAlira SakaLa
Gata dinamAnankapari Hni Heyu
JA Bhitare Tamekhali Tame HoithAa
Agapari/Ajipari KichhitA SwapniLa KichhitA Rangeen Hoi
Mo ChAripate BaLayahoi
DinarAti GhuribuluthAa
AsantAkAli Pruthibira Sabu AnischitatA Satwe
KhAli Mori Raktare RenuRenu Kari
Tama Nisswasara Gandha BharideuthaA

 							-Susmita Patnaik
