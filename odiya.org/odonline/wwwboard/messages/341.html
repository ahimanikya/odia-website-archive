SRI VISHNU SAHASHRA NAM PARAYAN TRUST
AT- MUKHTI MANDAP, PURI, ORISSA
As per the Divine Instruction of his Sri Sri Sankaracharya of Kanchikamakothi Pitham, The learned Pandits of Mukti Mandap of Lord Sri Jaganath of puri, Orissa, India have started regular Parayan of Veda, Sri Vishnu Sahashra Nam, and Sri Purusha Sukhta since 26th sep'99. The day of advent of Lord Shri Balabhadra. The spectulary holy parayana (Recital) on the Mukhti Mandap is having two sessions every day in the morning hours and evening and it shall continue for ever as a spiritual tradition for the welfare of humanity and mankind as decided Sri Vishnu Sahashra Nam, Parayan Trust bearing registration number 1568. The parayana by the learned Pandits of Mukthi Mandap and the its spiritual vibration has its colossal deep impact on the piligrimes who are visiting the Sri Jaganath temple Puri for darshan all over the world. The chairman of the trust appeals the spiritual mind people to donate liberally to this Registered Trust in the Account opened by the Trust Bearing A/C No. 5186 of Union Bank of India, Chandrasekharpur Branch, Bhubanceswar, Orissa, India for the continuance of this great Divine work at Mukthi Mandap, Puri, Orissa, India.
