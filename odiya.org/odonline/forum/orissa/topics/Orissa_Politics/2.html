Present Political Scenario in Orissa

Bikash · 1998-03-15 15:12:07 UTC

The Political Scenario in the state is miserable as the ruling Congress Party
has lost in most of the seats in the last Lok Sabha Election. Naveen Pattnaik,
the son of the Kalinga Veer Biju Pattnaik, has really come up by his tactics and 
his association with BJP. He rifted the opposition party Janta Dal by forming his
party Biju Janata Dal (BJD). Few of the big shots lost are K.P.Singh Deo,
Srikant Jena, Soumya Ranjan Pattnaik, Bhakta Das etc. We have added the up
to date information in our web site www.orissa.net which is launched very recently
and hope to expand its coverage. Pls. do browse and let us know your suggestion.
