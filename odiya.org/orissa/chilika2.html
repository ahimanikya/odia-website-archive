Valmiki Rishi, the author of the Ramayana, was a great man who was in his earlier life a thief and a hunter , who had killed all sorts of animals. His life had a great reformation when he came into contact with the Seven sages of the Indian Pantheon who then made him repent for this past sins and then after severe penance he emerged as s supreme soul who had realised divinity. He was blessed by the creator of the universe Lord Brahma to write the great history of the AVTAR of Lord Vishnu the preserver of the universe as Rama the king of Ayodhya. When Rama ruled the world there was no theft in the kingdom. People led their lives without fear and there was no poverty known. The rains were always on time and trees bore flowers and fruit in abundance and in season. Famine was unheard of and peace reigned the entire world. There was justice meted out to everyone with an impartial eye and men lived long lives. Untimely death was unknown and disease or illness ceased to exist. That was Ramrajya in brief.

Now to the Chilka lake. Valmiki was told that he would get the right time and inspiration to write out the story of Lord Rama. Once when Valmiki was passing through the jungle with his disciples he found a hunter shoot at a pair of birds. One of them died and wreathed in pain. The birds were in their mating mood and in their very couette activity. When the other mate found its partner wreath in pain , it came close to its dying mate and cried its heart out. All who saw it could be moved by such a sight. Valmiki Rishi could not control his grief and temper on seeing such a heartless hunter- Out of his grief ( Shoka ) he cursed the hunter and his outburst resulted in a poem which is called a Shloka . { The orgin of the word Ghazal lies in this etymological expression of the Ramayana . When a hunter shoots a fawn with an arrow the sound that the fawn cries out in pain of the arrow strike is called a 'Gaz' . The song that results from the pain in a poets heart is called a Ghazal !!)

Back to Valmiki again .... When Valmiki cursed the hunter the Shloka was born and then the Ramayana writing had begun. Now the primary question is - Even though Valmiki himself was a hunter in a previous life , why did he curse another hunter , who was in fact just like what he was in his early life ? What was the reason for the terrible grief and stirring of his emotions? ( all Rishis are supposed to be masters of their minds and bodies). Well the answer to this question is that these birds whom the hunter killed belonged to the 'Chakravaka' family of birds ( Please correct the spelling or the correct genus). What are these Chakravaka birds, the death of which moved Valmikiji so much ? Well thesegenus of creatures are so designed by nature that after they mature they always live in unique pairs. Thus a jodi or pair will always consist of a male bird and a female bird. What ever they do will be synonymous. They will eat together, swim together, fly together and shake their wings together, etc. When one of them dies a natural death the other always starves itself to death or even kills itself in sorrow.!. Thus this symbolises the ideal of true love and marriage or friendship and co-existence. Thus one may have Chakravaka ducks, animals like horses , birds and even human beings who would think the same way ( which is symbolised in the 36/36 horoscope comparisons of Indian Astrology). Now when the hunter shot these birds they were in the happiest moment s of their life. The intensity of sorrow that the bird was so great that it faced the most rude shock that can happen to any creature. In the normal course of life this bird cant bear separation, how intensely it must have sufferred when separated from its mate during its happiest moments !. Thinking as such Valmiki was greatly perturbed and accosted the hunter - How could u be so hearltess to kill such a creature ? And that too of the Chakravaka family ? ! Thus he had reasons to curse the hunter.
