(The most ancient university in Orissa, now non-existent, is probably the ``university of Ratnagiri'' mentioned in the famous Chinese traveller Huen Tsang's diary. Ratnagiri, 105 kms. from Bhubaneswar, was a seat of Buddhist religion and philosophy.)
