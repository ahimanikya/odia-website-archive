Bhubaneswar: the city of temples

Bhubaneswar is the capital of the state of Orissa. "Bhubaneswar" means the "abode of God" or "master of the universe"; and appropriately enough, it is referred to as the "city of temples" for its numerous temples.

The temples of Bhubaneswar record the Kalingan architecture from its beginning to its end.
