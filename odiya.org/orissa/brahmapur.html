Berhampur, the main commercial town of the district of Ganjam greets a visitor immediately on his arrival from the southern part of India and wishes him a happy sojourn to discover the eastern part. It is a convenient base point from where a tourist can plan his visit to various places of interest in the district of Ganjam.

      Gopalpur-on-sea conveniently accessible from here.

ARYAPALLI

30 Kms. from Berhampur and 6 Kms. from Chhatrapur, the district head- quarters of Ganjam, the beach at Aryapalli is quiet and charming where the waves talk. Its bracing and invigorating climate adds freshness to the soul. A nature loving tOUrist can enjoy the sun-surf-sand among the green casuarina plantations. 50 Kms. from Berhampur, amidst lush green forests is Taptapani where there is a Hot sulphur spring. The hot water is channeiised to a pond nearby to facilitate fascinating baths. Regular bus services operate to this piace from Berhampur. A two-roomed Panthanivas of the O.T D.C, Ltd., provides accommodation to the tourists (Reservation Authority, Manager, Phone- Digapahandi-21). A pipeline drawn from the hot spring provides the natural hot water inside the bath rooms of this Pantha nivas.

MAHENDRAGIRI

Amidst rich scenic beautY of the evergreen forests, Mahendragiri, 5,000 feet high, stands in its unrivalled glory in the Parla- khemundi subdivision of the district Of Ganjam. The hill is associated with mythological stories from the Ramayana and the Maha bharata. It finds mention in the Works of poets Kalidas and Radhanath Ray. The rivulet Mahendra Tanaya flows down the hill with her usual trickling note. Archaeo- logical remains at Mahendragiri make an interesting study for the scholars. Sivaratri is the main festival here which draws a large crowd. Mahendragirl can be approached from Parlakhemundi which is frequented with bus services from Berhampur (125 Kms.) via N. H. No. 5 or via Taptapani and R. Udayagiri (176 Kms). One can go from Parlakhemundi to Kainpur (46 Kms ) by bus and then to Burkhat pass (5 Kms.) by Jeep and finally proceed to the Mahendra- giri hill (10 Kms.) on foot.

TARATARINl

The temple of Taratarini. the twin Goddesses, is situated on the top ot a blue capped hill. The beauty of ,the place is enhanced by the encircling river Rushikulya. The shrine' draws huge crowd throughout the year especially on every Tuesday in the month of Chaitra. The place is 32 Kms. from BerhamPur frequented by regular bus service. There is a Revenue Inspection Bungalow here for accommodation whose reservation authority is Tahasildar, Chhatrapur.

JAUGADA

Jaugada forms a part cf the Malati range of hills and occupies an important place in Ashokan studies. Here on a clean surface of granite rock, one could find the rock edicts of Ashok which provides valuable information about the pattern of admini- stration followed by the great Emperor. About 2 Kms. from Jaugada is Buddhakhoi vvhere a visitor can see imagos of the Buddhist pantheon along with the Shiva shrines. There are regular bus services from Berhampur to Purusottampur (35 Kms.) from where the road up to Jaugada is jeepable.
