Historical corrections — Purna Mishra

I want to post a few corrections.  Shailadbhova dynasty ruled the ancient
Kamboj (Cambodia).  Most of the Oriya boats left for South East Asia from
three principal sea ports: Tamralipta (in Midnapur district of WB),
River port near Rusikulya Muhana (Somewhere between Gopalpur and Chilika),
and port near Kalingapatnam (I forgot the name).  There is no historical
data on Cuttack before Chodaganga moved the capital from Kalingapatnam
(or Mukhalingam) to Varanasi Cuttack.  The story we heard about Markat Keshari
and Baimundi has not been verified by the historians.  Many historians believe
the old city of Cuttack probably did not exist prior to Chodaganga's time.  Mostof the history books are quite silent on the his\sorry\ history of Cuttack.  Even Prataprudra Dev spent most of his life
protecting  the southern border of Orissa from the fort of Vijaywada.

Balesore became an imprtant sea port (even though it was on Budhabalanga
river) later.  "Baleswari Ponga Luna" by F. M. Senapati describes the
importance of the Balesore port and the commerce on it.

It is no surprising that the old name of Bay of Bengal was Sea of Orissa.

-- Purna Mishra
