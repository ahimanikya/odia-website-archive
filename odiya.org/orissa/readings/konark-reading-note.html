Konark reading recommendation — Suresh Baral

To those working on the Konark page and other intrested ornetters:
There is a book titled, "New Light on Sun Temple of Konark," authored by
Sadasiva Ratha Sharma & Rajendra Das, published by Alice Boner Publications of
Benares. This book has some interesting (some think controversial) account of 
Konark and the Dharmapada story. I have read this book, and I think it has some
answers to the Konark puzzle (particularly how it was built). The late Sri 
Gopinath Mohanty had the opinion that this book is authentic, but became a 
victim of politics. There are three extensive volumes by Thomas Donaldson of 
Case Western Univ. which describes Oriyan Temple architecture and documents
allmost all temples of Orissa. I suggest that Somedutt and others take a look 
at these sources. All these volumes are available at U.C. Berekeley Library. 
Hope this is helpful. Suresh Baral
