Odia Samaja Bandhu Milana 2006

IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT

To All,

Due to some unavoidable circumstances, our organizer of BM-2006 has to move to India.Since time is very short to reorganize the BM-2006 in elsewhere, BOA has decided to cancel this years BandhuMilana keeping in mind well being of its members & well-wishers. Hence it is unfortunate in my part to announce the cancellation of OdiSA's Bandhumilan-2006, which supposed to be held on 4th July 2006 at California. My apologies to all for the inconvenience caused due to sudden changes.

Requesting, our web management committee & public relation committee to do the needful in resolving all issues/concerns due to such unavoidable circumstances if necessary.

Thanx & Regards Biswaranjan Jena (CE/CA, OdiSA)

The Odia Samaja (OdiSA) is excited to announce that its forthcoming official get together known as Bandhu Milana-2006 will be held on Saturday, 1st July 2006 thru Monday, 3rd July 2006 at Los Angeles, California.

On behalf of oDiA samAja, the CE Biswaranjan Jena, and event organizers (Ahimanikya Satapathy, Bikash Panda) invite all odias in Americas. Please mark your calendar for July 1st - July 3rd 2006.

We have booked Banquet Hall in Ramada Plaza Hotel Anaheim Resort [Map] which is just 5 minutes walk from the Disneyland main gate for the whole day on July 2, 2006.

Those who are coming outside of LA, We have negotiated a special rate for Bandhu Milana 2006 attendees at the Holiday Inn Express [Map] located in the next building across the main venue in Ramada Plaza. Just mention the name Odia Samaja to get the special rate of $95.00 per night during Weekdays and $89.00 per night during weekends. This price does not include taxes.  [Book Rooms Online or call them 714-772-7755]

Bandhu Milana, just as the name suggest is dedicated to become a platform for odias in Americas to establish a strong ethnic ties. Bandhu Milana is an opportunity to strengthen existing friendships and make new ones. It is being designed to be an event full of fun filled activities for the entire family. The previous Bandhu Milana at Toronto went a long way in forging new bonds which will be renewed here. [Register Today]

Kids will have plenty of opportunity to show their talent. We are open to suggestion at this point. Artists this is the time to show your talent. The children's program is open and each child participant has the opportunity to make, at least, one stage appearance. Parents are encouraged to get their children ready.

We want to be fair to all our Bandhus who are coming to LA and want to give the opportunity to participate in the fun in deciding the list of things that we can do together in the July 4th long weekend. We are accepting suggestions and feedback till April 30th... so please watch the Events page in our web site for updates.

About Odia Samaja: Odia Samaja has been formed to promote bandhutwa and flourish our culture and language. OdiSA is an organization of friends across the globe, and is not restricted to any geographical or political boundaries. Odia Samaja has promoted very successful ventures like duradrusti (www.duradrusti.org) and Odisha Bikash Initiative (OBI: www.odiya.org/obi), that display its commitment towards promoting odia language and culture. For more details on Odia Samaja visit www.odiasamaja.org.

  

The menu is being considered carefully to make food at BM2006 a memorable experience. Our organizers are very creative and will enable you to experience a new taste of "Bandhutwa".

To complement all this Los Angeles is an excellent place for a holiday. The City of Angels. This fabled sprawling metropolis - famous for its celluloid history - is part reality, part dream. If you are visiting LA for the first time specifically with kids, you don't want to miss Disneyland, the happiest place on earth so we are working towards getting a group discount deal. Visited Disneyland before? We bet you will have tons of fun visiting again with all Odia Bahdhu!

We will also make arrangement for discount tickets for other attraction, please give us a call and we will help you plan for your extended stay.

We suggest you come as early as June 30th(Friday), at least plan to come by July 1st (Saturday) morning and plan to stay till July 3rd evening. Our volunteers will be present at Holiday Inn Express starting Friday afternoon. So please plan ahead. Give us a call today if you have any question. If you are local in LA and you don't want to stay in hotel, you still encourage you to come at least on 2nd July.

Looking forward to your friendship at Bandhu Milana 2006.
 
Truly Yours,
Biswaranjan Jena, Chief Executive, Odia Samja
