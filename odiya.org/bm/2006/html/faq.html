Q. How would the weather in Los Angeles area be during the Bandhu Milana?

A: You may expect a nice sunny weather during the July 4th weekend. The temparature that time of the year normally stays between 62 degree Farenhit (17 degree Celcius) and 86 degree Farenhit (29 degree Celcius)
