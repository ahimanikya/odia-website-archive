Location

Where to eat?

Gandhi Palace, a popular Indian restaurant located inside the Ramada Plaza Hotel will cater the food on July 2, 2006. Breakfast and lunch will be at the restaurant and the dinner will be served in the banquet hall. Attendees staying in the Holiday Inn Express will also get a 20% discount on food & beverages during their complete stay.
