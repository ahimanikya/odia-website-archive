Annada Shankar Ray


Born and brought up in Orissa. His origin is from Bengal. Now settled in Calcutta. Member of famous Sabuja-Juga poets. Has contributed to Oriya literature through numerous poems related to dreams. He now writes in Bengali. He also cleared Indian Civil Service. He is married to an american lady.
Source :Punya Palit [ppal@pyramid.com.au]
