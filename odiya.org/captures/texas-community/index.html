Research

My Ph.D. research involves the study of some fundamental aspects of designing asynchronous circuits, particularly the delay-insensitive (DI) ones. Major emphasis is given to novel models, techniques, and theory concerning design of extremely low-power circuits. (Click here to send me a mail and obtain a postscript copy of my dissertation entitled "Approaches to Design of Circuits for Low-power Computation".) This research explores a theory of delay-insensitivity and several optimization techniques for speed, throughput as well as power efficiency in such circuits. New power reduction techniques for adiabatic (clocked) circuits are presented. We have newly developed the theory of "conservative, delay-insensitive" computation as a fundamental basis for very low-power operation of circuits. Some technologies are proposed where such a theory seems most applicable for design of dense and/or ultra-fast circuits.
