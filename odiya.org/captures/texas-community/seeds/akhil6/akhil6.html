Sustainability, and self-sufficiency are the cornerstones of our philosophy. Hence we would strongly request you to include a few plant-nurseries and cut down a few village-schools in their place. We want to see people grow and take care of themselves and not be dependent on handouts. We also feel that it is better to do a few things well than to do a lot poorly. Therefore, we ask that you take up 15 `one-teacher schools' and 10 plant-nurseries attached to 10 of those schools. The nurseries should be built by the village beneficiaries themselves and should be tended by the students, too. Environment-friendly agro-techniques and development of means of earning through cooperative selling of produce/saplings should be properly emphasized. Although we can extrapolate the number of saplings that can be raised in 10 nurseries from your estimate of 14 nurseries in the original(first) proposal, please do send your educated estimate for the 10 recommended nurseries for our records.

Our grant and your local funds together add to about Rs. 171,000 and therefore, the budget above allows a slack of about Rs. 39,000 that could be used for any other essential or desirable additions to the project outlay in future (for example, starting a meal-program, adding 5 more schools, meeting travel-related cost, etc.)

We wish to consider the possibility of starting a simple lunch-meal program at a couple of schools. It could consist of rice, Dal, and a bit of curry or fruit to be used as an inexpensive but nutritious lunch and as an incentive, for the CHILDREN coming to the schools regularly and their teacher, on school days only. We encourage participation of the area people, students and agencies in contributing foodgrains, labor and other materials to the meal-program, to supplement any small grant we may provide. We seek your QUICK FEEDBACK on FEASIBILITY AND EFFECTIVENESS of such an experimental meal-program.

We wish to work with you closely as we like to actively participate in the kind of multi-faceted and long-lasting development that you envision to bring about. Our participation, as dictated by our great distance from Orissa, will involve periodic visits by our members and supporters to learn and know about your methods, successes and failures and contribute ideas and technology whenever possible. We might also send some development experts from elsewhere in Orissa or India to visit the sites and your center to write us reports that would benefit both you and us, in the long term. Some of us here in North America might even volunteer our time to work in the project area as humble workers... We are concerned people just like you and others, except that we live far away. Some of us are likely to visit you at the site in Nov., Dec. of 1993 and Jan. of 1994.

We will notify you of any visit by those trusted representatives who should be extended the privilege of seeing your records, financial or otherwise, relating to THIS project. We need these progress reports (and later, annual audit report) on this project, to in turn prepare our own reports to the donors of this project. These reports are ABSOLUTELY ESSENTIAL for continued funding by the donors and for maintaining our status vis-a-vis the taxation authorities here. Brief monthly reports to us should list the names of people hired, travel expenses, supplies, constructions, names of schools and nurseries, the names of teachers, number of students, sale of saplings, AND an estimate of money you need for the next two months, etc. We REQUIRE timed-reports and timed-expenses.
