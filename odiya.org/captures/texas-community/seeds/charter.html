Sustainable Economic and Educational Development Society (SEEDS)

SEEDS is I.R.S.-registered as a "social development-, social service- type" organization. The Employee Identification Number for SEEDS is 74-2723080. SEEDS is also officially registered as a non-profit student organization at the University of Texas at Austin, where it is based.

PHILOSOPHY and ASPIRATION

SEEDS philosophy is rooted in our wish to promote educational and economic progress and development for the common folks particularly in Orissa, but in the developing world in general. We, in principle, also would take stand against social evils and injustices whenever possible. We hope that these, in turn, would indirectly bring about and foster the other important ingredient -- political consciousness-- among the people.

We do not believe in simple, conventional charity. Rather we believe in working as perhaps catalysts and facilitators of development --- the exact roles for people near or in Orissa will naturally differ from those of people who are abroad. Fundamentally, we support and promote the sort of development that is

  • SUSTAINABLE (long-term, self-supporting, environmentally non-predatory),
  • EQUITABLE (fair and just, more for the needy), and
  • DE-CENTRALIZED (not imposed from above, grass-roots, native solutions).
We hope this this will eventually grow into a movement of "participatory consciousness for sustainable & equitable development" among non-resident Indians and Orissans.

WHEN DID IT START?

It started in March of 1993 in the wake of shocking news of inhuman misery and deprivation in the famine-affected region of Orissa, as a result of extensive discussion on a electronic medium called ORNET. Initially, it was informally called "Kalahandi-Bolangir Initiative". The effort was organized into an independent group in April, 1994. The group was formally named as "Sustainable Economic & Educational Development Society" (SEEDS), reflecting its broader focus and philosophy.

BRIEF, CONCRETE OBJECTIVES

We seek to be conscionable agents of sustainable, non-violent, democratic and equitable development in Orissa and elsewhere. Towards this end, we see the need to educate ourselves and other non-residents, to raise monetary contributions and ideas from among us to catalytically help development workers and ordinary people to help themselves achieve their freedom from hunger, deprivation, ignorance, and exploitation -- the many socio-economic & political ills. We donot have delusions of any quick, grand changes by a few of us, but we hope the movement grows among more people to steadily but surely proceed towards that lofty goal. The two facets of our goal:
  • We serve as ambassadors/messengers of the SEEDS philosophy. This is the awareness part. We bring to light people's suffering and inequity to help alleviate/eradicate the problem. Mobilize youths and others outside Orissa who will be forward-looking in this respect. Serve as an incubator for new and viable ideas of development.
  • We try to financially and morally support/adopt some small-scale, grass-roots development projects in Orissa, and later, beyond. This will give us (a) first-hand knowledge about the issues of progress, (b) volunteering opportunities, (c) concrete milestones to achieve, and more.

PLAN OF ACTION?

Information campaign among non-residents to know and consider modes of development for the disadvantaged region and its people. It could be done through publications, presentations and networking. That is, spread awareness and harness positive contribution to the cause of development. Undertake a few of development projects in the region(s) that have a clear and creative plan to achieve the KB-initiative's goal. Give stress on self-reliance and sustainability aspect of the project. Experiment to make any sound experience gained from the project to be widely reproduced elsewhere. Establish a rapport between the non-residents and the project workers & beneficiaries in order to motivate the young and the old to spend effective time at the project sites to teach and learn and to perpetualize the benefits of a raised consciousness and hard work.

HOW SHOULD WE MAKE SURE OUR MONETARY AID IS WELL-SPENT?

We should follow many different directions for checks and balances:
  1. First off, choose a "reasonably" reliable/reputable organization to work locally based on reputable recommendations, previous experiences, record, scrutiny of project proposal, etc. Darshan has prepared an extensive "grant application form" for project proposals and IPAG and other KB coordinators will review and extract independent opinions/info to do as well as they can under the circumstances.
  2. By ourselves doing the needed work to cut "middlemen" as much as possible in executing the projects.
  3. Since it wont be so remote to bigger towns of Orissa, we need a group of knowledgeable volunteers who would visit the site(s) and write reports or us, perhaps, with some compensation.
  4. Asking our friends/relatives whom we trust most to keep an occasional tab on the work there without being OVERBEARING or INSULTING to the actual workers & volunteers at the site(s)
  5. Insisting on a quarterly progress report and an annual audit report from the local group. We should even pay/grant a few dollars towards independent auditing expenses.
  6. By making our genuine interest in honesty and diligence abundantly clear to them and by predicating our phased funding on good/reasonable progress. We shouldn't give a false picture of rich, "charity" organization doling out money (to get rid of guilty conscience).
  7. By various volunteers among us communicating to them that we are one of them and each dollar we spend is our hard-earned money, not a charitable largesse.
  8. By a few of us spending a week or so (when visiting Orissa) at a project-site to volunteer our labor and toil with them. I believe it will be extremely hard for them to betray our collective goodwill.
  9. Don't expect a 100% return in our investment immediately. Be very patient.
  10. We have also contacted some reputable, well-known scientist/worker like Mr. K.R. Datye of Bombay and an organization like "Development Network" of Pune, who have expressed willingness to help us monitor satisfactory progress through their contacts & expertise.

HOW YOU CAN HELP?

We are seeking active participation of you for reach-out, for research on specific agro techniques and environment-friendly seeds, for verification of proposals and subsequent monitoring of projects, to volunteer time in future at a project site, or for educated discussion on alternate development methods suitable for Third World, etc. Last but not the least, although we suggest contributions on a continuing basis, any donation to carry out suitable projects is welcome. Also, please say how else you can help, as this is YOUR movement.
  • FUND RAISING and OUT-REACH: You can do so in your local areas, e.g., by motivating and appealing to potential donors through entertaining programs with education value. Asking a donor to take responsibility for a small project that we meticulously seek out. You can suggest a donation of $6/month from students and about $18/month from working people which amounts to their forgoing a single dinner at a restaurant a month. Any amount below or above the suggestion is just fine, but if the donors pay every now and then, say once every two months, the idea of SEEDS will take root in their mind. So they won't treat us as a charity organization. The philosophy is a growing, living concept, and money is just one important resource. Let people know that involvement is extremely important.
  • FIND or WRITE ARTICLES suitable to our goals so we put out for every one's benefit, and publish them in Orissa or abroad.
  • DO RESEARCH IN some agricultural or health-care techniques, e.g., that we can pass on to one of our project areas for experimentation.
  • SEEK high-integrity, young, social-minded individuals in Orissa (your area) to execute some well-thought out projects that the local impoverished people want -- under guidance of some experienced, competent leaders. Finding such proposals is very hard. SEEDS will try to raise funds and materials, educate & disseminate info, provide technical assistance, etc. from the North America -- if the project-proposals are promising and of sustainable (self-reliant) nature to be executed by honest and competent people.
  • YOU can help us in monitoring and detailed-reporting of on-going projects when you are visiting in Orissa/India. Some youths may even be interested in working a few weeks at some project sites.
  • FORM a sister or branch organization or join an existing one with a similar philosophy.

PROJECTS UNDERTAKEN:

We are currently executing a project involving 18 "one-teacher" schools and a scheme for raising fruit-saplings for a moderate self-sufficiency. A phase of volunteer-training at Bhagavatula Charitable Trust is completed. We are looking into production and application of environment-friendly, high-yield fertilizer from sewer sludge and industrial fly-ash. Other projects being explored involve low-cost housing, and "adoption" of tribal girls for their school expenses, etc. Please see the WWW directory mentioned above for more details about the projects, and other matters.
