------------ Oct 3, 1994 -------------
Today's news from the Vanabasi Kalyan Ashram (VKA) project at
		various villages of Lanjigarh block in Kalahandi
		*****************

Suresh Mishra is project-in-charge. They have 3 whole-timers instead of
two; their names are Sri Kamalesh Kumar, Sri Tarun Dash and Sri Mohan Munda.
No. of schools they have scouted to establish is 18 in ten Gram Panchayats.
They expect 35 students, who had no means to go to schools, in each of
our school. The school will open for 3 hours/day; the exact time to be
determined at each local village.
Teachers' training camp starts in Oct. for 10 days.
Schools will inaugurate in November, 1994.
They have given a relatively brief accounts statement stating that they
have received Rs 43,500/- from us in two installments. Their expenses
till end of June, 1994 is Rs. 12,943/-

They have sent us a few colorful one-page pamphlets on their work in
Orissa. I will be happy to send you one if you wish to share.

------------- Addendum on Oct 12, 94 ------
One important piece of news from the letter is that the Lanjigarh block area
where VKA has been active and also the area in Phulbani where they have their
prior activities was left out miraculously from the inter-class riots and
killings that ended just couple of months ago.  Earlier, there was news about
some horrible riots and loss of life in an Oriya news-paper.
Bharat Agrawal, who is the over-all organizer of VKA
in Orissa, attributes this fact to in part to the social influence of VKA
activities in the area.
