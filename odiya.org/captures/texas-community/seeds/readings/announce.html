Friends,

This is to remind you again that I have made available all
pertinent info on SEEDS at the World Wide Web (WWW) site
	http://www.cs.utexas.edu/users/darshan/SEEDS/

Some people with mild to high interest in SEEDS have joined to communicate
nitty-gritty detals on our seeds short email list.
Important public info (e.g.,
project update) is put on the Web site. Occasionally I or someone else
keep informing on Ornet if we have matters that may interest the public.
Then there is the newsletter, Ankur, and our occasional slide presentations
at gatherings, etc.

including Debasmita, who have asked.  Those of who cannot use mosaic/netscape
because they work on non-graphics terminals, there is a text-oriented
web-browser called "Lynx".
