The Editor,




Dear Sir,

We are a small group of young students, professors and other
professionals  from the USA, Canada, and Japan interested in
supporting grass-roots socio-economic development  in  rural
Orissa.   We  were functioning informally for a year. Now we
are organised into a group called ``Sustainable Economic and
Educational Development Society (SEEDS)''.

We wish to promote economic and  educational  well-being  of
the  poor and the disadvantaged through small-scale projects
undertaken by Non  Governmental  Organisations  (NGOs).   We
strongly  emphasize  participation  and  involvement  of the
beneficiaries while our support will be as caring catalysts.
The  projects should be `sustainable', that is, the projects
should have the potential to be nearly self-sufficient in  a
few  years;  the  benefits  of a project should be long-term
even if, say, only a small village is benefitted.  We  favor
projects that do not degrade the natural environment.

We are currently funding a project  spread  over  15  tribal
villages  in  Lanjigarh  block of Kalahandi. This involves a
few one-teacher schools providing functional education and a
few fruit-plant nurseries promising economic sustenance.  We
are also about to sponsor  and  begin  training  of  several
young  volunteers  from  different  parts  of  Orissa at the
renowned social-worker and agro-specialist  Dr.  Parameswara
Rao' Bhagavatula Charitable Trust.

This year we will be able to generate a little over  1  lakh
rupees  to  fund  suitable  and  creative  projects fully or
partly.  Proposals for the same may be sent at the  earliest
to the address below.

With kind regards,
