ANNEXURE-I
BACKGROUND & NEED
I. Most alarming global problem is the population explosion now a days. 
It is more acute and headache for all developing countries. India is not 
an exception to it. Till to-day we became able to protect about 43.5Vo 
of Eligible Couple(E.C) by the methods of birth control or in other ward 
the Couple Protection Rate (CPR) in National level in India is 43.5%. 
The target area which is chosen for this Project comprising with the 
population 1,11,830 in 150 Nos. of undeveloped rural villages of Naugaon 
& Balikuda Block in the District of Jagatsinghpur of Orissa. This target 
area now surveyed and found the existence of 15,806 eligible couple, out 
of which only 3,256 couples were protected by permanent method of birth 
control. The CPR of this target area is around 22% much below the 
National level.

II. The geographical situation of the villages of this target area are 
miserable due to poor transportation facility, lack of road 
communication, non-existence of rail-head and existence of many brackish 
water river and canal. The target area situated within the 10 Kilometers 
areal distance from the beach of Bay of Bengal. So the natural 
calamities like drought, high floods and cyclones are regular and common 
features of the area.

III. Financial conditions of the people of this area is under below 
average and about more than 65% of the population are under poverty 
line. There is no small or medium or big industrial unit in this area. 
The principal occupation of the 950% people is cultivations and day 
labourers. There is no ample scope to provide labour to the day 
labourers. So many people starves and half-fed.

IV. Due to the geographical and financial condition of the target area 
this gastrisational disease are very much common. The epidemic outbreaks 
normally in every year.

V. Though the area is having standard facilities f o r education yet the 
people at	large are not aware of sanitation, nutrition, immunisation, 
safe- drinking water and environment.

So we propose to undertake the responsibilities through implementation 
of this proposed Project stated as under
(i) to protect at least 75% of the eligible couple by birth control 
methods and to raise the CPR at least up to 60% to 70%.
(ii) to create massive awareness to fight against epidemics and in-take 
of safe drinking water and habituated with sanitary latrines.
(iii) to motivate for kitchen garden concept in every holding and aware 
the people about nutritious foods around them.
(iv) to motivate for mass immunisation and to arrest the child mortality 
within 30 per thousand from prevailing 110 per thousand.
(v) to educate the people not to devastate the mangrove and fight 
against the prone cuture maphia (?LM: prawn culture mafia?) from 
encroachment of the forestry land, adjacent to Bay of Bengal.
(vi) to built a cadre of volunteers and trained them to undertake the 
above and plant one tree per year at least in each household.

2. PRELIMINARY WORK DONE BY THE ORGANISATION

I. A detail Survey of the target area on health, population control, 
sanitation, environment, safe drinking water, natural calamities and de-
afrostation (?LM: deforestation?) have been completed.

II. 100 numbers of tubewells with high platforms more than 5 feet from 
ground level now being installed by the Organisation out of which 45 
tubewells has been completed and rest works are in progress and expected 
to complete by a couple of months. This sources shall be able to provide 
safe drinking water for the target area even if at the time of high 
flood.

III. The Organisation has taken steps to set up a sterilisation hospital 
with indoor & outdoor facilities and with a mobile medicare unit. The 
hospital building comprising with a carpet area 9,000 sft. for the 
provision of 40 beds indoor facilities. The ground floor of the building 
as on now has been completed. Expect to complete the civil work and 
equipped by the end of the March'96. This hospital cater the needs of 
population control, preventive, diagnostic and curative health services.

3. NUMBER OF BENEFICIARIES

1,11,830 services planned for people to be benefitted.

4. TIME BY SELF SUPPORTING

The Project shall be self supported after 3 years and the funds can be 
generated out of the health services provided in a nominal price.

AGE SERVICE

We have already set up an Old Age Home at Balikuda f or 25 inmates 
in the last financial year and this year also we have established

now rendering existing service by rehabilitating 50 helpless destitutes

of five numbers of Day Care Centres and one Mobile Medicare Centre at 
Balikuda & Naugaon Block Area. The proposals are pending with Ministry

DRUG ABUSE

We have made a great deal of exercise in identifying the 
potentiality of addicted persons in different places of the State and 
finally submitted the proposal to undertake deaddiction and counselling 
centre in Cuttack, Puri, & Bhubaneswar Special Jail and for which we 
have been permitted by the then Inspector General Police(Jail) but due 
to some technical difficulties we have finally decided not to proceed 
with the programmes in jails.  We however, preferred Paradeep for 
implementing the programmes and accordingly submitted the proposal. The 
proposal was considered by Ministry of Welfare, Govt. of India and 
sanction was accorded to undertake the Awareness and Counselling 
Programme for Jagatsinghpur District with Head Quarters at PaWadeep. Now 
the Awareness & Counselling Centre is functioning at Paradeep 
successfully. The outcome is highly satisfactory. You will appreciate 
that there has been immense demand for running one deaddiction centre at 
Paradeep within this financial year.

SPECIAL SCHOOLS FOR HANDICAPPED

We have made a survey of the handicapped children at Jagatsinghpur 
District and sought for assistance from the Ministry of Welfare, Govt. 
of India to set up three Special Schools at Jagatsinghpur, i.e. for the 
Mentally Handicapped, for the Hearing Handicapped and for the Visually 
Handicapped. In the mean time, we have received the sanction and 
approval from the Govt. of India to run one School for the Mentally 
Retarded Children and the School has started functioning with effect 
from March␒95. We are awaiting the sanction of other two Special Schools 
from the Ministry. Further, we have chalked out a proposal to provide 
Vocational Training (?LM for different Handicapped beneficiaries?) for 
their the Ministry of Welfare after being duly recommended from State 
Government and NIRTAR, Olatpur. 

SC/ST PROGRAMME

During the current financial year we have submitted Project for SC 
& ST (?LM?) beneficiaries which have been duly recommended by the State 
Government and the Director, National Commission for SC/ST, Bhubaneswar, 
These Projects cover trades like Computer Training, Mobile Medical Care 
Services, Pre-Coaching Centre at Jagatsinghpur, Naugaon and Balikuda. 
None of these Projects has been sanctioned as yet.

FLOOD RELIEF

We have involved ourselves in relief operation at the time of 
unprecedented flood which has shaken the financial backbone of the 
people at Jagatsinghpur. Flood water remained for a period of two months 
during which drinking water became a problem. It provoked us to file a 
proposal to provide safe drinking water at the time of flood, to the 
people. The proposal envisages installation of tubewell with high level 
platform in resting places of the flood victims in Naugaon & Baikuda 
Blocks. We have suggested such type of tubewells in the affected area 
and opened dialogue with Caritas, The Netherlands, to assist us in this 
Programme through CENDERET of XAVIER Institute of Management. At the 
time of my presentation of this report, I am happy to inform you that 
such Netherlands funding agency has been pleased to sanction us 50,000 
gillders to undertake such Programmes. We expect funds at early day of 
the coming financial year for our flood relief and drinking water 
resource programmes. In view of such sanction, the basic, need of the 
people of the area can be fulfilled. I would like to convey my 
obligations on behalf of you all to Prof. S.P. Das, Professor & Head, 
Prof Dr. S.S. Singh, CENDERET of XAVIER Insitute of Management, 
Bhubaneswar.

has been pleased to release us grants-in-aid amounting to Rs.50,000/- to 
create awareness on nutrition, small family norms and population 
control. We have successfully launched such programme by way of creating 
awareness by way of Padayatra among the school students, Seminars, 
Symposium, Public Meetings and staging drama etc.

We have submitted a proposal for opening 75 Creche Centres at

HEALTH SERVICES & POPULATION CONTROL

earmarked by the State Govt. for the construction of the hospital 
building is very less.
