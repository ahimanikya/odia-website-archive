To
Mr. Priyadarsan Patra				May 23, 1994
SEEDS


Sir,

Kindly refer to your letter of May 5, 1994 addressed to Dr B. V. Parameswara
Rao regarding the training of six youths at our site. Keeping in mind
your requirements, we have developed a module covering the the course 
content as indicated in the annexure. The training will be imparted in English.
It is a residential course and the charges would be Rs 100/- trainee day. 
Thus the total cost on training of six people for 21 days would work out to 
Rs 12,600/-. The course can be commenced within 15 days after receiving your confirmation to this.

Our site is located close to Haripuram village, which is 10 km away from
Yellamanchili, PIN - 531 055, Visakha Dist, AP.

Looking forward to your confirmation.

yours sincerely,

(RAGHAVA RAO)
Co-ordinator, Training.

ANNEXURE:

BHAGAVATULA CHARITABLE TRUST: YELLAMANCHILI
-------------------------------------------

Objectives of the course:
------------------------

1. To get insight into problem of proverty.
2. To get an understanding for sustainable and equitable development.
3. To know methods, techniques of agriculture and spirit of development.
4. To get to know the organisation of people for their own development.

Sylabus of the course:
---------------------

1. Situational analysis	: Village people - Analysis - Proverty - Types,
			  Causes & alleviation methods.

2. Resource generation	: Resources - Man, Land, Animal, Water.
   and mobilisation	  Thrift, Saving - income generation.

3. Management of 	: Community organisation - Types of formation of Mahila    Human Resources	  Mandals or Youth Groups - Animation, Qualities of
			  functions of Animator - Leadership Generation.

4. Natural Resources	: Appropriate Techniques - Waste Land Development -
   Utilisation		  Biogas - Bio-intensive Gardening.

5. Social communication	: Songs - Role Plays - Audio - Vedeo Methods.
   Techniques

6. Social Development	: Health, Education, Literacy - What, Why, How ?
			  First Aid, Cheap Nutritional Food.

7. Monitoring 		: Principles and Techniques.
   and Evaluation 

8. Field Visits		: Village Poor Family, Social Forestry, Waste Land 
			  Development, a Voluntary Organisation, Mahila Mandal.
