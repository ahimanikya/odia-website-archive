Here is a series of items I have saved from various sources--most of them opposing huge dams planned for the Sardar Sarovar and Narmada Sagar projects in India. The opposition is due to claimed deficiencies in (1) the environmental safeguards, (2) resettlement & rehabilitation of the oustees, or (3) economic soundness associated with the dams.
