A Personal Perspective -- Abani Patra

Several weeks back in the middle of a very cold day in Buffalo, I offered to put together this issue of Ankur, partly to offset the boredom of a new place, partly to assuage my conscience for not having done much for SEEDS over the last year and partly pity for Darshan's workload! My involvement with SEEDS has always been for reasons that are completely selfish. Maybe I am less of a man than a lot of my friends, but anything that is not immediately personal rarely moves me to action. The whole charity and altruism thing simply does not work for me. I will talk at length about the horrors of Bosnia but I do not go out and fight their cause. Hunger in Kalahandi or Bolangir are still reasonably abstract concepts to me. I empathize greatly with their misery but I doubt I would have ever done anything (not that I am doing much now !). However pity for Darshan's workload and boredom are powerful and immediately personal motivating forces.

A little bit about this whole sustainability thing. As I said before, I do not believe in the whole altruism thing (too much Ayn Rand when young) so if I am going to help anyone they better be helping themself or at least be getting into a position to do so soon. SEEDS is not and will not become a charitable organization. We sometimes come close to that when faced with great misery but our focus is not charity. We have neither the resources nor the desire to dod so.

Every great effort I suspect is led by a few genuinely great souls -- the Gandhis and the Martin Luther Kings -- and a host of others with more immediate personal reasons for joining the effort. At this juncture of the evolution of SEEDS, I feel we have enough of the great souls (at least two that I can count), what we now need are more foot soldiers like me with immediate personal motives. If I may misquote a JFK, ``Ask not what you can do for SEEDS, ask what SEEDS can do for you''. I personaly guarantee you rewards beyond your wildest dreams. It even makes great cocktail party conversation! So please, find your own reasons but do join us in whatever shape or form you can. If nothing else is possible, at least give us your money. We would like more but we will settle for that.
