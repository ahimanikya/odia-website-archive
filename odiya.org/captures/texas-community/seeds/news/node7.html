Conclusion

Time and again we have emphasized that money is merely a means, and not the "the be all and end all." SEEDS activists are still a very small group hard-pressed for the time resource. We need your ideas, criticisms, and involvement to keep SEEDS alive. We need your help in obtaining, screening, evaluating, periodic monitoring and reporting of projects and proposals. Of course, we also need people for out-reach and fund-raising as well. Our task has been difficult, and twice as much because we must meet on the network or by phone. What makes it even harder now is SEEDS derived most activism from the student members in the past, and most have graduated struggling to find footholds in their careers as well. Of course we could have chosen to do nothing as an alternative to this demanding yet profoundly rewarding task. So, we need your involvement more than ever.
