Sambalpur

Sambalpur, the socio-cultural center of western Orissa was built in 17th century by the Balaram Sai Deo, the then king of Koshala and was named after Goddess Samaleshwari. Sambalpur came into political prominence in India when the great freedom fighter Veer Surendra Sai sustained his fight against the Britishers for a pro-longed period of 14 years. Sambalpur is considered as the cultural, education and political center of Western Orissa and hence the language(dialect) spoken in Western Orissa is known as Sambalpuri. Sambalpur has a University, an Engineering College and a Medical College (all are situated in a place called Burla) beside Gangadhar Meher College which is a premier institution of Orissa. There are few places of tourist interests near Sambalpur which includes the temple of Maa Samaleshwari, the Leaning temple of Huma, the Ghanteshwari temple of Chiplima and the Hirakud Dam complex. Hirakud Dam is considered as the longest dam in the world with a length of 25 KM. Gandhi Minar (a revolving tower) and Jawahar Minar are situated on the either sides of the Hirakud Dam adding beauty to the dam. Gandhi Minar gives a nice bird s eye view of the Mahanadi reservoir and the adjacent mountains and jungles. The hand loom sarees are one of the greatest contribution of Sambalpuri weavers to the Indian Heritage. Barapali (currently in Bargarh district), considered as home of Sambapuri Sarees is around 80 kms from Sambalpur. Barpali is also the birth place of Gangadhar Meher, one of the greatest Oriya poet of all time. Khinda (currently in Jharsuguda district), the birth place of Veer Surendra Sai is 40kms drive from Sambalpur town. Nrushinghnath temple with beautiful ever-flowing fountains on the Gandhamardan mountains is about 170 kms from Sambalpur and Ushakothi game sanctuary and Pradhanpath water fall (currently in Deogarh district) are around 160kms from Sambalpur town. The greater Sambalpur district (now divided into Sambalpur, Jharsuguda, Bargarh and Deogarh) is full of mineral resources like Coal, Lime stone, bauxites, graphites, etc. Sambalpur is directly connected to New Delhi, Madras and Calcutta by express trains and Jharsuguda is well-connected to all metros by train communication and is only 50 kms away from Sambalpur and well connected to Sambalpur by train and bus. Sambalpur has very good bus communication with Cuttack and Bhubaneshwar. Sambalpur is famous for Seetala Shasthi Jatra which is organised in early June every year. Navanna (popularly known as Nuakhai) is one of the popular festival of Sambalpuris which is organised the day after Ganesh Chaturthi. Sambalpuri folk song and folk dances are very popular in all parts of Orissa. -- Contributed by Debabrata Panda (dpanda@sequent.com)

Places of Tourist interest in Sundergarh district :

  1. Rourkela

    (Contributed by Debabrata Panda)

    There is a nursery rhyme in Oriya that reads: "Rourkelare Dina raati, Gadhaa hue luha kete Jati . (which means "Rourkela is busy day night, With making of Iron of many kinds") Rourkela (in the Sundergarh district), the steel city of Orissa came into existence in the late fifties with the implementation of first five year plan of India and the tribal land culminated into a beautiful township. Rourkela boasts one of the most modern and largest steel plant of India built with a Indo-German collaboration. Rourkela, situated on the bank of River Bramhani is the second largest city of Orissa with a population of around 500 thousands. It has the distinction of having 100 per cent literacy. A lot of small and big industrial units have come up around Rourkela since its inception. Kanshbahal, Kalunga, Rajgangpur are the few other industrial towns close to Rourkela. Regional Engineering College, Rourkela is one of the premier technological institution of India. There are few places of tourist attractions near Rourkela which includes Ved Vyas and Mandira Dam. Ved Vyas is considered as a sacred place for Oriyas and is considered as the birth place of Ved Vyas, the author of The Mahabharata. The Ved Vyas is very famous for Shiva Ratri Festival. Ved Vyas temple is situated near the confluence of three rivers Shankh, Koel and Saraswati to form River Brahmani. The Indira Gandhi Park of Rourkela is the largest park of Orissa. Rourkela is well-connected to all metros of India by trains with excellent communication facilities with Calcutta. Rourkela is also connected to Bhubaneshwar by air.
