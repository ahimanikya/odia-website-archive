Review topic: Announcement about starting Odiya Samaja

Friends: We are starting an Odiya Samaja (OdiSA) and that would be an organization of Non-resident Odiyas. We are envisioning to have chapters in various countries where we have communities of Odiyas. While there would be no central administration binding all chapters, but we might have ambassadors who would work as liaison. If we all join together, we could achieve a lot. A first thing would be to organize cultural forums, linguistic activities, leadership, etc. We could work together to help each other and also help Orissa when needed. Please join us. We have started a mailing list and you may support the movement by joining the list. Please send an e-mail to: [email address omitted] We are not chartered yet, but will be pretty soon. So, please inform your friends and be a part of the history. Be a chartered member. You may visit our website to get more information.
