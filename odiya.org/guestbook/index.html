Voices from the Oriya Home Page guestbook

The saved guestbook contains 917 dated entries from 1998–2003. The earlier reading preserves all 17 March 1998 entries. This stage adds 40 later entries chosen for what they record about community organizing, contributors, language access and the experience of using the site.

Selection method and limits

All 917 dated source segments were parsed. Of the 900 after March 1998, two keyword screens identified 87 candidates. Forty entries were selected and read in full for this edition. This is not a full substantive review of all 900 later messages, and the selection is not a representative survey. The remaining 860 entries are not judged to lack historical value.

Selection focuses on named contributor acknowledgments, organizing announcements, language access, content requests, community uses and practical or critical feedback. Generic greetings, advertisements, individual contact searches and intimate personal messages were not selected. The sparse 2002 and 2003 selections do not imply that the website had fewer visitors or less activity in those years.

Three similar messages by Basudev Padhi on 23 August 1999 and two by Sujagya Das Sharma on 14 December 2000 are retained and labelled. Their differing wording is not silently merged, and no edit history is inferred.

Signature email fields, author-link destinations and separate location fields are omitted from the later readings. Six addresses within four messages are replaced by “[email address omitted]”. Historical web addresses in message text are plain text. The visitor host/IP log is not included.

Displayed dates and poster names are source values, not authenticated dates or identities. Visitors’ views, recollections, claims and notices remain attributed to them. They do not independently establish launch dates, legal status, relief shipments or the implementation of requested features. The source’s historical “Oriya”, “Odiya”, “Orissa” and name spellings are preserved.

The original guestbook link on odiya.org opens this new selection guide; it is not the complete original guestbook or a capture from the referring page’s date. Earlier Columbia guestbook addresses are not reassigned. The original files, article drafts, timeline and approved homepage article are unchanged.
