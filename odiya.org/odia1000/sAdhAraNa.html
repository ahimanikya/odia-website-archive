This page is a compilation of the most commonly used Odia words.  The words used by any humnan being in any language can be divided into three levels.

  1. Level 1: The usage of this set of words will make a person indistinguishable from any real common speaking Odia in daily normal contacts.
  2. Level 2: Knowledge of Level 2 words wil let it be obvious to the observer that the person is a well-read educated gentleman ("bhadrabykti").
  3. Level 3: A repertoire of Level 3 words would be what would distinguishes a literary person from the rest of the populace.
