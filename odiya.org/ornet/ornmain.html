REGARDING ORNET ( FAQ )
*********************************************

Ornet is a mailing listserver for of ODIYA's all over the world. The articles and discussions are mainly pertaining to our motherland (ORISSA), our problems, solutions, News etc.

This medium consist of professional gentlemen and women of all ages. So please be considerate in your type of posting. Usage of slangs is never appreciated.

A request to post your own constructive original articles.Please DO NOT use this medium for any kind of personal conflict or misunderstanding.

ORNET was initiated by Subrat Mohapatra in 1989 alongwith a very few acquaintances in the mailing list. Currently, it is being maintained and managed by Ashutosh Dutta. The total number of members as of April 24, 2001 is 768 and are growing everyday.


IF YOU ARE A NEW MEMBER TO ORNET, PLEASE COME FORWARD TO INTRODUCE YOURSELF WITH YOUR NAME, BACKGROUND AND YOUR ORIGIN IN ORISSA. THAT WAY WE COME TO KNOW EACH OTHER.

ABOUT ORNET LISTSERV

It works at cs.columbia.edu as any other Mailman listserv. The process is automatic and does not require anyone's intervention.

TO SEND AN e-mail TO ALL MEMBERS OF ORNET

send your mail to

ornet@cs.columbia.edu

and your article get distributed to everyone. Please remember you need to subscribe to the mailing list in order to be able to post an article in ORNET

TO ADD A MEMBER TO THE ORNET DISTRIBUTION LIST

Please inform the new would be member to access the ORNET INFO PAGE and use the Subscribing to Ornet form.

In order to subscribe directly from your e-mail account please send an e-mail to ornet-request@lists.cs.columbia.edu and type "Subscribe" in the message area.

You may subscribe to ornet in the digest mode whence you shall get about one mail per day from ornet where all the postings of ornet are appended together as one single mail.

You may be able to subscribe for the digest mode by accessing the ORNET INFO PAGE and using the Ornet Subscribers form field.

TO UNSUBSCRIBE FROM ORNET:

Please access the ORNET INFO PAGE and use the Ornet Subscribers form field.

Please do not send e-mail to ornet@cs.columbia.edu requesting anyone to UNSUBSCRIBE you.

TO SEE THE LIST OF RECIPIENTS OF ORNET

Access the ORNET INFO PAGE and use the Ornet Subscribers form field using your e-mail id and password.

There are a lot of other things you can browse about ornet by accessing the ORNET INFO PAGE
