Subject: Asish's thoughts


I was pleased to read the candid observations from Ashish.
People refuse to understand India because it's too difficult
to grasp.  Some people contemplate and make their own theories.
It is the charge with the new young men not only to discover
the heritage, but to tread the beauty of its magnificence.

Let me request everyone to learn some Sanskrit and get
overwhelmed by the Indian scholarship.  Through the search,
you will also bump into the splendid contributions from an 
area which has been lately called Orissa.  The learning
will be peaceful and you will feel blessed!

We have a lot to cover.  Let's get moving.. 

- Bijoy Misra.

Subject: Weekend Thoughts from Ashish Mohanty

Dear All
Weekend Thoughts (10/20/95) that came from the young energetic and brilliant 
mind - Ashish Mohanty of Minneapolis, was very..very interesting to me.
Particularly impressive were his experiences : 
   "The majesty of the Taj Mahal was just unreal while the public hospital 
   rooms with overworked doctors rushing to save the many people dying was 
   saddening.  I realized that this was what made India so beautiful.It then 
   dawned on me when I was at the Taj Mahal that One cannot truly appreciate 
   the beautiful till one has seen the hopelessness & despair."
 
And of course his comments on his own learning of Hindi :
	"I am relearning Hindi by taking a class on campus.
         My teacher is a Bulgarian. Imagine that! A Bulgarian
         teaching Hindi to a Indian in America. Only in America
         can this happen!"

Thanks to Monty Mohanty and Wishing all the very best to him for everything
that he attempts in his life.

And.... to all of you on ornet........

"A WARM WISH FOR THE COMING DEEPAVALI"

Regards

Vishvabhusan Pati
