We could provide equipment such as an incubator for Pediatric Nursing since taking a baby to SCB Medical College Hospital is extraordinary costly,> Rs.4000/-per child. The estimated cost of nursery equipment is around> Rs.1-1.5 lakh($4500/-). We believed we could raise the amount in USA.

Drs Mahamaya and Jogamaya Patnaik have undoubtedly done a great amount towards providing a better life to orphans and elderly they have taken under their care.

I had a personal remark about how "extraordinary costly" it is for pediatric nursing or other care in SCB Medicial College.To my best belief all government hospitals in Orissa are supposed to be at no cost to the poor and needy ,and also others of substantial means. Real expenses to a patient at e.g. S.C.B medical College are supposed to be usually true costs of supplies and medicines not available at the hospital for numerous reasons (legal and illegal) including just lack of adequate resources.A cost of course which nobody seems to be interested in dealing with is illegal payments demanded by doctors at these hospitals for use of tax payers facilities (the so called private practise).Several top physicians in the state demand such fees for inpatient services provides at the government hospitals or in private facilities,something illegal by state law.Adequate diagnostic and therapeutic facilities are woefully inadeqaute and have been monopolised by top professors for personal gains.Actually last I heard there was no incubator for premature at SCB Medical College.

It may not be very difficult to provide a watchdog like service and help improve some government health services in Orissa by interested NROs , at least at some of these public institutions Many of us have used or have relatives who depend on these state government health facilities.A stategy of such activities could easily be worked out if there are sufficient interested individuals.The state government health care system still provides a service to mankind in Orissa far beyond what any private institution in the state has done or can ever expect to do.Some outside monitoring and nurturing may improve the existing infrastructure significantly without a tremendous effort or expense.

I apologise for the long message and would appreciate any feedback on starting such a activity.

M.K. Patnaik
Los Angeles
