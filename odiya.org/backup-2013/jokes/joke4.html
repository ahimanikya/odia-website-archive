JOKE # 4

Lawyers..Lawyers...Lawyers....


Q. Why does California have the most lawyers while New Jersey has 
   most toxic-waste dumps?
A. New Jersey got the first pick.

Q. What's the difference between a lawyer and a vulture?
A. The vulture doesn't get frequent-flier miles.


Terrorists hijacked an airliner full of lawyers on their way to 
a convention.They threatened that unless their demands were met,
they would begin releasing one lawyer every hour.


A lawyer appeared at the gates of heaven andd began loudly 
protesting that he was only 42 - much too young to die.
"That's funny", St.Peter replied. "According to the hours you've 
billed, we thought you were 93".

--
