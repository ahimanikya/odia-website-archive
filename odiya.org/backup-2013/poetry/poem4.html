NAnti TAra RupA

by

Tusar Dash Mohapatra

Note from the Author

The following poetry is dedicated to those who are married/living-together/planning to marry in near future..
Hope you all will like this.

Eka komaLa sparsha ra Aghata re nidrabhanga hue,
dhire dhire Akhi khole mu aTi jaTana re,
jhulipadithibA kesha mandhye chanda badanati
KaLa baDal majhire jahna pari Unki maruthae
Prema mishA muruki hasa chhAi Alua ra kheLa,
KajaLa lepiTa taNA taNA nayana re
Surjyodaya ra ankiTa engiTa,
Otha Duiti kakarajukta sabuja ghAsa pari
SakaLa ra surjyodaye chak chak karuThae,
Kana ra pendiphula duiti manDira ra
Ghanti ku praTiDhyanityua kare,
Gala ra laLimA surjya ra praThama kiraNa re
Nutana ankuriTa kamaL sama dhala dhala karuthae,
KakarajukTa chak chak sabuja ghAsaku chhuin chhuin,
KamaLapurna puskariNi re snAna karu karu
KarjyaLaya jibAra samaya byaTibyasta kare
abadhya pila tiE mAra koLaru bAhAri
BidyALaya abhimukhe chhaLa chhaLa nayanare jiba pari,
Mora karmarata dibasara shubhArambha huE, Tara nayanara DurE,
Rupare aTuLaniya, naanti Tara RUPA.
 				-Tusar Dash Mohapatra

Ete Swapna PAiN RATi NAhiN

by

Tusar Dash Mohapatra

Note from the Author

Here's a poetry which I had written while I was a graduate student. Hope U will like this. Thanks.

Mu huanTi Krushna sehi Gopapurara
ChorAi khAAnTi labaNi , huaanTi labaNichora,
CharAi jaaNTi gaii Brundabanaku,
KhelanTi luchakALi mo priya RadhikA ra sathe,
GadhajAnTa kabiTabaLi mAna abhimAnara.

Mu huanTi Prithvuraja Itihasara
Uthai nianTi SanjukTa ku jor kari,
LagijanTa khandajudha jyain swashurara.

Mu huanTi pradhanamanTri Bharatbarshara,
UthijanTa asakhyaraTa , bekarira nama
pheriAsanTa Ramarajya punithare samasyAku bheDi,
GadhanTi swadhina, eka purna swadhinara dhama.

Mu huanTi bihanga mukTa Akashara
bhrami bhrami Desha DeshAntara gaanTi
ShanTi , Prema o bhaTruTa baNi,
Swadhina mu purna swadhina.

Mu huanTi kabi eka jubaTi manara,
RachanTi kabiTA mo priya ku nei,
MahAnadi balukA shajyA re,
jyostnAbharA purnimA rAtri re,
hansa hansi keLi kalabeLe,
Se huanTa prema ra KabiTa..

 				-Tusar Dash Mohapatra
