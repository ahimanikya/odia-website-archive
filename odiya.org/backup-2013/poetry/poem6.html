Breaking the Silence

by

Pradeep Kumar Lenka

Tumeki sehi sura mo besurA kanthara
Tumeki sehi chhanda, chhandahina mo jIbanara

Tumeki marujhara mo jIbana sAhArAre
Tumeki se surabhi mo mana upabane

JIbana AkAshare bindu tume prathama barshAra
Mo smruti shrAbana gahan nidare chhanda_ki tume nupurara

Tumeki se saru muruchhanA mo mana bINAra tAre
Tumeki saja phula phutichha sate_kibA mo manara abujhA se sabuja banare

Mo mana AkAsha_tale tumeki sei janha
Mo AshAra ilAkAre sehije padachinha

Tumeki sehi mora adhAdekhA sapanara smruti
Tumeki sehi....... sate.......tumeki sehi sAthi.

 				-Pradeep Kumar Lenka
