Anabana Kabita

by

Debabrata Panda


Note from the Author

Some of these are the couplets from my published poems with different Names in some Oriya Magazines/ broadcast by All India Radio.

Pahili Kirana

Sedina Farcha Akasha ku Chaahin 
	Saakshi Rakhi Chauthi Janhaku
Shapatha karithila Tume Sajibani Jalapari taye |

Jeebanara Naksaa aanku aanku pachaarili Shabdataara Artha boli
 	Kahinki lajeyi gala
Maapi na thili tuma Othara Ushnataa,
Maagi na thili tuma Sannidhyara  Muhoorta gudaye |

Di Paada

Satare Kana Anubhavi huye
              Aakhira Gabhirataa Prathama Dekhare |
Maapchup Kari Huye Samparkara Lamba Prastha
	      Aantarikataara Akuha Ojana |
Sedina ta Kahithila Tume Bujhi paara Mookankara bhaasha
Padhipaara Chandrare Kalanka;
Kahinki je byartha hela Mora Muka bani karibaku Prema Nibedana |

Tinoti Shabda

Samparkara Sabdate Kahidiyaje Phulate Phootijaau,
Nacheta Abhimaan bharaa Chahanitaye Aanki diyaje Mo Chhaati bhitarata 
Phaati Jau |

Upasanhara

Kahilani thare,
Aani thaanti Tuma paain Chandra Tara
Kini thaanti Basanta daskhinaa pabana |
Binchi thaanti Golapa pankare 
Kahi thaanta jadi thare
Se tinoti shabda tumara se chayiti Othare |

 				-Debabrata Panda (Debu), Beaverton, Oregon
