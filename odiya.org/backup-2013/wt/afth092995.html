Refinement of the model proposed by Umakant:
---------------------------------------------

The model was for the person who is DEAD !!

Redefine:
	T(i) = PROPORTION of time spent TILL NOW for ith degree of freedom

Therefore, we should have T(1) + T(2) + .....+ T(N) = 1

similarly redefine Hi(T) as 

	Hi( T(i) ) = PROPORTION of happiness due to ith degree of freedom.

This model is dynamic as T(i) is going to change as you age !! Also 
now it takes care of unit problem of Happiness as mentioned by Manoj 
Sahu. Since Hi ( T(i) ) is a measure of proportion, it does not matter 
what is the unit of measurement !! So each person should find out what 
makes him/her happy and determine Hi ( T(i) ).

Please let me know if this refinement is O.K.

than on any other Hi.

Certainly H1(T=0) is not infinite for BillG. May be H1(T) = nT where n
