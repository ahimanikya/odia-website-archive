Good stuff Dr Baral.

I really appreciate y'r concept of heroes. I have done my I.Sc and B.Sc.(Physics) from BJB College, BBSR. I know y'r heroes pretty well. They are also my heroes in my educational life. Dr PK Satpathy and Dr RK Panda were really selfless teachers who were not offering tuition, when tuition was a business for most of the teachers in maths dept in a college where most of the students come from a rich background. Dr RKP and Dr PKS were very honest, strict and simple-living. I remember Dr RK Panda used to wear white clothes only, and some fellow students call him 'traffic police'. DR RN Panda was very fond of his students and he could do anything for them. But due to pressures from some rich and high-positioned parents, he was offering tuition. Dr RK Panda worked as Controller of Exams at Utkal University. Dr PKP and Dr RKP are the most honest teachers I have ever met. I remember, I used to go to Dr PKP's house for asking Maths doubts in My I.Sc time for free. It's really great time in my days at BJB college with most of my favorite teachers. I appreciate Dr Baral's portrayal of the great teachers in favour of of all netters who know them.

Thanks & regards,

Tusar Dash Mohapatra

A great article and a fitting tribute to the teachers who are behind the success of so many Oriyas. But I have once comment to make :

While a teacher may be an excellent teacher, as far as feeding knowledge to his/her pupils, I, personally *will not* adore them as great teacher, when I have serious doubt about his/her character, especially when it is harmful to the society. In the article by Dr. Baral, one such teacher was very highly praised, I do not have an iota of respect for that person with such a cheap character.
