Let me first state our enjoyment on Sri Panda's portrayal of Lakshmana in kaLiyuga ramayaNa. This group was talented..

His observations on the feasts in our urban areas are revealing. it's sad commentary on our sociology and values. People's behavior on such occasions does indeed get strange.

When we were young ( in upper grade high school), we would visit most weddings in Cuttack in search of good food! Since we knew people, they would give us more food than we could eat. I had a friend who would fall sick of overeating.. This stopped totally when we were in college and I remember attending only some of our family social occasions only. Some nostalgia..

I have been told that the trouble in the social occasions has been the arrival of many unannounced guests. The host is expected to treat all people who arrive. It becomes a false prestige issue on the part of the bride's parents since most of the unannouced people are bridegroom's remotely known relatives.

Earlier this month, we were in a wedding ceremony of a girl of Gujrati origin who married a boy from New Jersey. The girl was a student of mine in the local Indian cultural school. So I was a special guest. The occasion was held in a Function Hall with limited sit-down arrangement. While the arrangements were made for 500 people, about 20 extra people showed up who had flown all the way from India. Life of course became suddenly difficult for the hosts. The entire event was held off for two hours in order to create solutions to the problem.

The opulence of feasts in India has bothered me on the wastes. A lot of waste also occurs in our own local social meeetings. Since a third of the world's population still lives in hunger, the vulgarity of showmanship has to be protested in the quarters who might listen..

Today many of local associations will celebrate GaNesha PujA. Do partake what Ganesha eats and distribute some. I for one can't forget the great laddus we used to get in school on the puja days. We looked forward to GaNesha Puja year after year..

Bijoy Misra
