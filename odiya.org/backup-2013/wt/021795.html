PHILOSOPHY
============

Marxist: What's freedom. This word doesn't exist in the dictionary.

Leninist: Yes, you have freedom within this four walls of 3'X3'.

Capitalist: You are free to do whatever I tell you.

Socialist: You are free to do anything as long as I am not involved.

Dogmatist: I might become widow but my husband should die.

Pragmatist: Do whatever you want to. It's not going to be of any use anyway.

Egotist: I am the law. I make the rules. You better follow it or else...

Hypnotist: You think you can do this without my snap...eh

Conservatist: My forefathers did this, so I will follow suit.

Hi.. How about some articles on the column "Weekend Thoughts". I know anyone can post an article with this title. But it will be more fun to send it to me so that I might include some of my ideas and if it needs some clarification then we can ask people to clarify it before the weekend. This will help keep the ORNET a bit alive.

How about an article for the next weekend...(misra@maroon.tc.umn.edu)
