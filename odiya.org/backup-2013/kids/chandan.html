My Introduction

I am Chandan Khandai. I am the eldest son of Saurjya Kanta and Ananaga Manjari Khandai. I live in Naperville, Illinois with my parents and younger sister Ankita. I go to Georgetown Elementary School. I belong to Grade 3B of Mrs. Baum's class. My hobbies include writing poems, drawing and painting.
