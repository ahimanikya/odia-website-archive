Prafulla Kumar Jena


Academic:
B.Sc, M.Sc and Ph.D. in Chemistry from Utkal Unversity.
M.Sc. in metallurgical engineering, University of British Columbia.

Employment History:
Senior Scientist, Bhabha Atomic Research Center, Trombay.
Professor in Metallurgical Engineering, Banaras Hndu University, Varanasi.
Director, Regional Research Laboratory, Bhubaneswar.
Acting Director General, Council Of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) in 1986.
Visiting Professor

Catholic University, Rio de Janiero, Brazil
Tohuku University, Sendai, Japan

Distinguished Professor of Tata Chair, IIT Kharagpur.

Prof. Jena is an internationally renowned metallurgist and has published numerous research articles in reputed journals. Recipient of various awards such as National Metallurgist award, Padmashri and FICCI (Federation of Indian Chamber of Coommerce and Industry) award.
