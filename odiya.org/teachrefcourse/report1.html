Teacher's Refreshers Courses in Bhadrak and Jajpur


Friends

I hope this new year must be wonderful for all of you. Wish you all a very happy and prosperous new year. Here I am reporting some small stuff we were able to acomplish during this period for information to the group. It is well known fact that high school curriculum has changed a lot particularly for Science with introduction of college level courses. However, there is no or little formal training or orientation provided to the teachers to enhance their skills for the advance courses. Most of the teachers feel handicapped in their class rooms in teaching those new curriculum, hence educational standards are degrading day by day. As a continuation of our efforts from the 1st teacher's refresher course for high school science teachers in Kendrapara District during last summer, this time we conducted two courses in Bhadrak and Jajpur District during Dec 26-29, 2001.

Both the courses were jointly sponshred by Jagannatha Organisation of Global Awareness (

JOGA)(www.jogaworld.org) and Odiya Samaja (ODISA) (www.odiya.org/myodisa. Response from the teachers, the resource persons and the organizers were overwhelming . We got dedicated and well-experienced group of college faculties and computer professionals to teach the courses in general science like, physics, chemistry, biology & introduction to computer science. Resource persons were available to the teachers to clear their doubts and to answer the questions they had from on any difficult sections form the school curriculum. The discussions in side and out side the class rooms were lively and thought provoking both for the teachers and the resource persons. The subject selections for the programme, course materials, methodology and valuable inputs from the resource persons were highly appreciated by the teachers ,which were clearly reflected in their end of course evaluation sheets . With the special request from teachers to know more about computer, the refresher course continued for third day in Bhadrak. Mr. C.S. Mohanty CI of Schools, Bhadrak was the Convener for Bhadrak District and Mr. G.C. Mishra, CI of Schools, Jajpur Convener for Jajpur district. 92 teachers attended the orientation course for Bhadrak and 65 for Jajpur.

Mr. Bhagabat Behera, Minister for higher education, Orissa deserves our special thanks for his supprt. I would take this opportunity to extend my sincere thanks to Dr. Sarat ch. Das, Reader in Chemistry Salepur College, Dr. Sudhanshu Tripathy Reader in Chemistry Ravenshaw College , Mr. C.S. Mohanty, CI Bhadrak, Mr. G.C.Mishra CI Jajpur and Mr. Biswamber Mishra Headmaster Jajpur Zilla School for their valuable time and efforts put together to make these courses sucessfull. Dr. Digambar Mishra & Dr. Jyostna Mishra from USA visited the refresher course in Jajpur district.

We are plannig to organize similar course in Khurda, Kalahandi, Bolangir, Jagatsingh pur and Sambalpur Districts in coming days, please come forward and extend your support for this noble cause.
