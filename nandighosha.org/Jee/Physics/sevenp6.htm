Universal gas constant R = 8.31 J/mole-k
Avogadro’s number NA =6.02 x 1023 mol-1

             Unless otherwise mentioned take atmospheric pressure as 1.013 x 105 Pa

Prob No. 1
                  A monoatomic gas, assumed to be ideal, has specific heat at constant volume as 0.075 cal/g. Find out mass of an atom of the gas?

Prob No. 2
              Assuming that air contains 23% O2 and 77% N2 (volume/volume) only. Calculate speed of sound in it at NTP. Molar masses of O2 and N2 are given as 32 and 28 g/mole respectively.

Prob No. 3
              100 cal of heat is given to a particular ideal gas due to which its volume changes from 1L to 2L at constant pressure of 1 atm. Calculate adiabatic exponent of the gas.

Prob No. 4
              A spherical containe of radius r has inlet at the top and an outlet at the bottom. The container is filled with water to half its capacity and rest of the volume is occupied by air at atmospheric pressure. Now inlet is shut and outlet is opened. When water stops draining out, it is found that the level of the water in the container has fallen to r/2 height. Calculate value of r. Given

                                            atmospheric pressure = 1.0 x 105 Pa
                                            gravitational acceleration = 10m/s2

                                  (Assume no air ingress into the container from outlet and temperature of the air inside to                                    be equal to that of atmosphere of the air inside to be equal to that of atmosphere.)

Prob No. 5
               A small tube fitted with a valve conects two thermally insulated vessels: A and B. capavcity of vessel B is double the capacity of vessel A. Initially vessel A is filled with a gas (adiabatic exponent =5/3) at 300 K temperature and 2 x 105 N /m2 pressure where as vessel B is filled with another gas (adiabatic exponent =7/5) at 350 K temperature and 4 x 105 N/m2 pressure. The valve is now opened and the system is allowed to achieve equilibrium in temperature and pressure. Calculate new temperature and pressure. Assume that gases A and B do not react when mixed.

Prob No. 6
              A perfect gas traverses through a cycle consisting of adiabatic, isobaric and isochoric processes in that order. Its volume during adiabatic process increass k fold. If ratio of molar specific heats at constant pressure and at constant volume is γ, prove that efficiency of the cycle

                                              η = 1 – [(k-1)/(kγ-1)] γ

Prob No. 7
              A thermally insulated cylinder filled with a gas at 300 k is held horizonantally. A thermally conducting piston that can move freely is at the middle of the cylinder. Cylinder is now slowly rotated and brought to vertical position. It is observed that the volume above piston is 7 times the volume below the piston and temperature of the gas has risen to 395 K. What is the value of adiabatic exponent of the gas?

ANSWERS:
Prob No. 1 6.605 x 10-6 Kg
Prob No. 2 331.1 m/s
Prob No. 3 1.32
Prob No. 4 220/27 or 8.15 m
Prob No. 5 342.6 K, 3.371 x 10 5 N/m2
Prob N0. 6 No Answer
