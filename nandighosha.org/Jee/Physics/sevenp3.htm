Problem No. 1

       Refer the figure which shows arrangement of blocks of masses m1 and m2 attached to massless springs of spring constant k1 and k2 respectively. Springs are in relaxed state and floor on which m2 is resting is frictionless. The coefficient of static friction between blocks is μ. Show that max’m amplitude of the SHM executed by the sysytem such that no slippage between blocks occur will be

  • [2μm1(m1+m2)]/|(m2-m1)(k1+k2) – (m1+m2)(k2-k1)|

 

Problem No. 2

       A constant spring of length L, mass m and spring constant k is suspended from its one end. Due to its own weight, to what length will the spring stretch to?

 

Problem No. 3

       A block of mass m=2.5 kg from distance d=1m from a horizontal spring starts moving towards it over rough horizontal surface with velocity v. Find max’m value of v such that the block doesn’t retreat after it has compressed the spring.

  Given that:
Spring Constant of the spring K = 320 N/m.
Coefficient of friction (static) μs=0.4
Coefficient of friction (kinetic) μk=0.3.
