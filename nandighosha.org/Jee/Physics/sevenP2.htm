Prob no.1

There is light source inside a glass sphere at ‘d’ distance from the centre. Find maximum value of ‘d’ in terms of ‘r’ (radius of the sphere) and ‘m ’ (refractive index of glass) such that no rays of light directly from the sorce experience total internal reflection.

    1. R/(1-1/m )

    2. (m -1)/(m +1) ´ r

    3. (m -1) r

    4. Ö (1- r/m ²)

    5. none of the above

 

Prob no. 2

A concavo-convex lens is placed (with it’s concave surface up) over a plane horizontal mirror. Positions of object and image coincide when former is held 72 cm above the mirror. If the "space between mirror and lens" and "lens cavity" is filled with water turn by turn, positions of coincidence of object and its image are 360 cm and 1080/17 cm respectively. Find the value of refractive index of lens material. The same for water is given to be 4/3.

    1. 3/2

    2. 4/3

    3. 5/3

    4. 0.333

    5. none of the above

Prob no. 3

A converging lens of focal length 'fo' is having one of its face silvered and behaving as plane mirror. If the other face was silvered it would have behaved as concave mirror of radius of curvature '2fo'. The radii of curvatures of concave and convex surfaces respectively are:

    1. fo and fo/2

    2. 2fo and fo

    3. 4fo and fo

    4. fo and 2fo

    5. none of the above

Prob no. 4

An optical fiber cosisting of glass core (refractive index 'μ') surrounded by coating (refractive index 'μ') is carrying a beam of light comprising of rays parallel to axis. Find limiting value of radius of arc, if the fiber is bend into, all the light will be carried through the bend. Given, diameter of cone = 2r.

    1. µ/(1+µ´) × r

    2. (µ´+1)/(µ´-1) × r

    3. (µ+µ´)/(µ-µ´) ´ r

    4. 2µ´/(µ+µ´) ´ r

    5. none of the above

 

Prob no.5

How high above earth’s surface one has to move so as to be able to view 1/3rd of earth’s surface area? Ignore refraction due to air and assume earth to be perfect sphere of radius ‘Re’.

    1. 2√2 Re

    2. 2Re

    3. √3/2 Re

    4. (√2+1)/(√2+1) ´ Re

    5. none of the above

 

Prob no. 6

A light bulb is suspended above the surface of water (refractive index = µ) in a swimming pool and a large mirror is place on the bottom. If bulb is lowered at the rate of 'x' m/s, at what rate the level of water in the pool must be decreased (may be by draining water at suitable rate) so that the separartion between the bulb and its image formed by the system remains unaltered?

    1. (µ-1)/(µ+1) ´ x

    2. (µ+1)/(µ-1) ´ x

    3. µ/(µ-1) ´ x

    4. (µ+1)/µ ´ x

    5. none of the above

 

Prob no.7

If the refractive indices of the three media are in harmonic progression, show that the focal lengths of a lens, when immersed in the above media turn by turn, will also be in harmonic progression.
