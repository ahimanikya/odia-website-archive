1.) Two identical balls are held together at rest touching each other. Along straight line which is a common tangent to these balls, approaches another ball towards them with a speed of u and elastic collision occurs. After collision all the three balls are found to have same magnitude of speed of v. Their directions are such that each one is 120° apart from the other two. Show that
                                                          v=u/3
 If the balls (initially at rest) are made up of material of density d can you find out density of the material the remaining ball is made up of?

A. 598 nm, .324 x 10-23
B. 598 nm, 3.324 x 10-19
C. 598 nm, 1.234 x 10-20
D. None of these

2.) In a photoelectric experiment stopping potential of 0.72 V and 2.35 V were noted against light frequencies of 7.2 x 1014 Hz and 11.2 x 1014 Hz respectively. Ascertain Plank Constant. Use this value to calculate work function and cut-off frequency for the target material.

A. 6.52 Js, 2.26 eV, 5.64 x 1014 Hz
B. 12.26 Js, 4.52 eV, 11.32 x 1014 Hz
C. 3.26 Js, 1.13 eV, 2.74 x 1014 Hz
D. none of these

3.) In an excited hydrogen atom electron falls to next inner orbit. The resulted radiation is incident upon a metal surface of work function of 1.03 eV. Photoelectron ejected from the surface having maximum possible kinetic energy is subjected to move perpendicular to magnetic field of 3.125 x 10-3 T. The photoelectron describes circle of radius of 1 mm. Which orbit did the electron fall from? Take ionization potentiahl for hydrogen as 13.6 eV

A. Orbit with n = 6
B. Orbit with n = 3
C. Orbit with n = 7
D. None of these

4.) Hydrogen atoms in ground state are excited by means of monochromatic radiation. Longest and shortest wavelengths in emission spectrum are 2.92 x 1015 apart. Find
 (i) wave length of the incident monochromatic radiation,
 (ii) number of lines possible in the emission spectrum.


A. 975 Å, 6
B. 363 Å, 8
C. 696 Å, 9
D. none of these
