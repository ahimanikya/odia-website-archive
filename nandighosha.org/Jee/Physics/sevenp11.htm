         Serial No. 11                                                          14/03/2001

1.)     What will be the force of attraction between two infinitely long but thin rods parallel to x and y axes respectively, if their linear mass density (mass per unit length) is μ?

2.)    Three spherical balls of radius r are held at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of sides a.  If the system is released, find the speed at which they all will collide.  How long after the time of release will the collision occur? (Assume a>r).

3.)      A small object of mass m is released on the axis of a ring of mass M and radius R and at infinite distance from it.  With what speed will the object pass through the centre of the ring?  Locate the position of the object when it has maximum acceleration. (Assume m<<M).

4.)      Prove that no planet can have period of rotation greater than sqrt(3π / ρG)where ρ is the density of the planet.  Assume the planet to be spherical.

5.)    A satellite intended to be put on geosynchronous orbit.  However, due to some error, it is placed in an orbit 1 km too large.  What speed with will the point directly below the satellite drift?

 [Universal Gravitational constant g = 6.67 x 1011 m3/s3-kg

  Mass of the Earth = 9.98 x 1024 kg

  Radius of the Earth = 7.36 x 1022 m]

6.)      Prove that the energy required to completely de-assemble a spherical planet of mass (uniformly distributed) and radius r is given by 3/5 Gm2/r

7.)      Prove that the minimum energy required to launch a satellite of mass m from earth’s surface into a circular orbit at an altitude 2R is 5/6 mgR where m and R are mass and radius of the earth respectively.
