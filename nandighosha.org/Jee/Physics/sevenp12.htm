SEVEN P (12)

 

Problem1

Mercury (density = r) drops of masses m1 and m1 are given charges q1 and q2 respectively. Find the energy the system has stored if these drops are kept far apart. Compare this with the energies the system will have if (i) these drops to form a single drop, (ii) a conducting wire connects to these drops momentarily.

Problem2

Two capacitors having capacities C1 and C2 connected in series are charged with the help of a battery having emf V. Now battery is removed and the capacitors are connected in parallel manner. How much energy does the system of capacitors lose?

Problem3

How many dielectric slabs of thickness t will have to be introduces between two parallel plates separated by d having air between them in order to increase the capacity of capacitor formed from its original value C0 to Ck. The dielectric constant for the slabs is given as k.
