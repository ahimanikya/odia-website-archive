Prob 1.

             An aluminium wire (length =6m) is joined to a steel wire (length = 8.66m). The compound wire is now held stretched under tension between two fixed supports. Now many nodes at least will be created if the system is excited to set up standing waves such that a node is observed at the joint. Assume, both aluminium and steel wires have identical area of cross section.

      Given:     Density of steel = 7.8 g/cm³. Density of aluminium = 2.6 g/cm³.

Prob 2.

            A man "A" with a vibrating tuning fork in his hand is running towards a masive wall with a speed of 4m/s. Another man "B" who is behind "A" is also running towards the wall with a speed of 6m/s. Calculate beats per second heard by "B" when (i) he is behind "A" (ii) he is gone ahead of "A". Also calculate beats per second heard by "A".

 

Prob 3.

            Two sinusoidal waves having wavelengths ë1 and ë2 are moving along the same line in the same direction. If d is the distance between two points where both the waves are in the same phase show that

                                          n/d = |1/ë1 – 1/ë2 |                     n = 1,2,3,....

Prob 4.

            Two ropes of different lengths having linear mass densities µ and 2µ are joined to form a single rope. No matter which end you attach to the ceiling to hang this compound rope, time taken by a transverse wave to travel across the length of the compound rope is the same. What ratio the lengths of the original ropes were in?

Prob 5.

            A race car which is moving on an circular path blows whistle continously. A man standing inside the circle observes that the ratio of minimum and maximum frequencies heard by him is 1:2. The ratio of the speeds of car and sound is 2:3. If the man is at x distance from the center, find out the radius of the circular path.

Prob 6.

            v1, v2, v3....vn are velocities of sound in hypothetical gases G1, G2, G3....Gn respectively. Find exprssions for velocities of sound in mixture I and mixture II. Mixture I is prepared by mixing identical volumes of all gases where as mixture II is prepared by mixing identical masses of all the gases. Assume that all the measurements have been taken at constant temperature and pressure. Also assume the adiabatic exponent of all gases to be same.

Prob 7.

             A block of mass is attached to the free end of sonometer wire. The wire was found to have fundamental frequency fo. If the block is immersed in water and in unknown liquid turn by turn the fundamental frequencies are found to be fw and fl respectively. Calculate densities of the block and the unknown liquid.

Answers :
            Prob 1: 8 nodes (nodes at ends included)
            Prob 2: Data insufficient (if we take speed of sound as 340m/s & freq of a vibrating fork as 100 Hz
                       (i) 3.64 Hz (ii) 4,81 Hz (iii) 3.61 Hz
            Prob 4: No matter what ratio the lengths are in waves travel for given conditions will always differ.
            Prob 5: 2x
            Prob 6: vI = √(n/( 1/v12 + 1/v22 + 1/v32 +......1/vn2))  
                       vII = √(( v12 + v22 + v32 + .......vn2)/ n)
            Prob 7: f02 / (f02 - fw2 ) and (f02 - fL2 )/(f02 - fw2 ) (g/cm3)
