1.)     A loop of the shape of a square of side a with one of its sides replaced by semicircle carries current i. Find the magnetic field at the centre of the diameter of the semicircle?

2.)    A system of a conical pendulum operates in a region of uniform magnetic field of B directed vertically upward. If the bob whose mass is m carries a charge q and the chord makes an angle of θ with the vertical find out period of the pendulum.

3.)     A particle of mass m and carrying charge q is admitted from bottom with negligible speed into an electric field developed by means of two (top and bottom) parallel plates at a potential difference of V and separated by a distance d. What minimum value of magnetic field in the horizontal direction must be applied so that the particle is prevented from striking the top plate?

4.)     A circular loop formed with a flexible and stretchable wire of length l at relaxed state has been kept in a horizontal plane in the region of uniform magnetic field B directed vertically upward. If i current is allowed to flow through the loop find the length the it will stretch to. The wire when stretched obeys Hook's law and has 'spring' constant of k.

5.)    A spherical shell made of non-conducting material charged with uniformly distributed q charge. If it is rotated about its diameter as axis with and angular speed of /omega/, find the magnetic field at the centre.

6.)    A very long thin walled cylinder (radius r) carries current i in the direction of its axis. Find the pressure exerted on the wall due to the current?

7.)     A long horizontal conducting strip of width w carries uniformly distributed current i along its length. Find magnetic fields at d distance above and below the line passing through the middle of the strip?
