The saved downloads page names autocalendar.xls; the available file is spelled autocalender.xls. This same-folder calendar is offered as a labelled spelling-variant alternative, not a proven identical download.

All 576 month-grid cells were read from the original cached records and matched to a disposable conversion; all 365 dates were independently checked. Year: Calendar!I10. Credit: Calendar!K8. The original range label says 1900–2078; that operating range has not been tested. The saved year is not a publication date. No original macros or live controls are included.
