Seven electrostatics problems and their saved answers. Problems 4 and 5 refer to diagrams that are not visible in the document rendering or the corresponding saved HTML. No replacement figures have been drawn.

Preserved worksheet text

Note: εo  permittivity of free space,     εr   relative permittivity of medium concerned

Prob No. 1)

A ring of radius r having +q1 charge uniformly distributed is held on a horizontal plane. A small ball having –q2  charge is released at the centre of the ring which beforebegining to retreat upwards falls down upto a point where intensity of electric field due to charged ring is maximum. How much does the ball weigh?

Prob No. 2)

Two rings of radius r and 8r are held on the same plane with their centres coinciding. They are charged such that the potential at the centre is zero, At what distance from the centre along the axis the intensity of electric field be zero?

Prob No. 3)

A tiny bob of a simple pendulum of length L is charged with +q. If +q and –q charges are placed at 2L distance dirctly below the point of suspension turn by turn, periods of oscillation are found to be T+ and T-  respectivel. Show that period of oscillation T when all charges are removed is given by:

2/T2  = 1/T+2  + 1/T-2

Prob No. 4)

A sperical shell of radius r has been cut into two identical halves which are separated by a distance r along the common axis of symmetry (as shown). If left and right halves are charged with +q/2 and –q/2 respectively, find out difference of potential at A and B where A and B are centres of left and right halves respsctively?

Prob No. 5)

A spherical shell of radius r has a portion of it removed such that points on the edge make angle θ at the centre with the axis of symmetry(refer diagram). .σ is the uniform positive charge density over it. If a point of mass m and charge +q is released at the centre, what will be its speed after it has tracvelled a distance of r? (ignore effects of gravitation).

Prob No. 6)

Three point masses A, B and C are held at (0,a), (0-a) and (√3a,0) respectively. Obviosly they are at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of size 2a. A and B charged with +q each. What charge (sign & magnitude) and acceleration (magnitude and direction) must be given to C so that, if system is released, the masses continue to be on the vertices of equilateral triangle with its sides unaltered? Ignore effect due to gravitation and assume all th points to be having mass m.

Prob No. 7)

Two tiny conducting balls of identical mass each charged with +q are suspended with non conducting threads of length L from the same point. Due to repulsive force balls are separated by a distance x. What will happen if one ball is discharged? What wiil be new equilibrium separartion in terms of x? Prove that the separation will be restored to original value if length of threads is increased to 4L.

Answers:

Prob No. 1: 2/√3 x 1/4π εo εr  x q1q2/r2

Prob No. 2: √20 x r

Prob No. 3: No answer

Prob No. 4: (√2-1)/4∏ εo εr x q/r

Prob No. 5: [2σr/m x 1/εo εr x (sin θ/2 – sin2 θ/2)]1/2

Prob No. 6: charge =-2q, acceleration = √3/m x 1/4∏εoεr x q2/r2 i

Prob No. 7: new separation = x/(41/3)
