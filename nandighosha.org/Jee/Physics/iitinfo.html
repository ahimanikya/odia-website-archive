Eligibiligy :

1. Age : 17 to 21 year. Uppear age limit is relaxable by 5 years for candidates belonging to SC/ST.

2. Candidates must have passed the final examination of X+2 system or equivalent with a paper each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Those appearing in X + 2 or equivalent examination may also write JEE for consideration of provisional admission provided they can submit the proof of having passed the examination before September 30, of the year of admission.

3. Candidate seeking admission to 5 1/2 year integrated M.Tech. course in Biotechnology at IIT Khargapur must also have passed in Biology in addition to Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics of X + 2 system or equivalent examination.

4. Those appearing in final year examination of three years diploma awarded by State Board of Technical Education are also eligible to apply.

Pattern of Examination : Papers in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, each of three hours duration. Question papers are in Hindi and English only. But you can answer the questions in Assamese, Bengali, English, Gujarati, Hindi, Tamil or Telugu.

For qualifying for admission a candidate must qualify individually in each subject. The merit list is prepared on the basis of aggregate marks in all the three subjects.

Reservation of Seats : At each Institute, 15% and 7.5% seats are reserved for SC and ST categories respectively. A separate merit list for SC/ST candidates is prepared applying relaxed norms. SC/ST candidates failing to qualify with relaxed admission norms but satisfying certain minimum requirements may be offered admission to a preparatory course of one year duration. Candidates successfully completing the preparatory course may be offered admission to the first year in the next academic year without going through JEE again.

Preferential allotment of Institute and courses : Two seats in each Institutes for Children of Defence/Paramilitary personnel killed/permanently disabled in action during war or peace time operations. One seat in each institute for physically handicapped candidates who are otherwise fit to pursue the course., DS and PH candidates should qualify in JEE in General Category (GE).

Direct Admission of Students Abroad (DASA) :

To be eligible for direct Admission, the foreign applicant must have

(i) Passed qualifying X + 2 class examination from a public school/board/university with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as three separate papers; or passed in any foreign country an examination which is recognised by the Association of Indian Universities as equivalent to the final examination of X + 2 system with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics as three separate papers; or Passed High School Certificate Education (GCE) Examination (London/Cambridge/Sri Lanka) at the Advanced (A) level at Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

(ii) secured the highest grades in at least two of the three subjects namely, Physcis, Chemistry and Mathematics and second highest grade in the third subject in a five-letter grade system. All other letter grade system will be normalised into the five-letter grade for deciding the eligibility. Where marks are awarded, the candidates must have secured at least eighty percent of the aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics with at least seventy percent marks in each of these three subejcts.

(iii) Taken SAT-II tests in Mathematics Level II C, Physics and Chemistry. The applicant should request Educational Testing Service, Princetion, PO Box 6200 , NJ 08541, USA to send directly to the Organising Chairman, JEE India under code 0035.

In case of an Indian candidate, the qualifying X + 2 examination have been passed abroad only.

In addition, a pass grade in Biology/Life Sciences at 10+2 level is also necessary for admission to Biotechnology at IIT Kharagpur.

Regional Engineering Colleges

Admission to 17 Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs) located in different States is regulated as per procedure notified by the Department of Education (Ministry of Human Resource Development) New Delhi in January / February. The prescribed application forms for admission to undergraduate degree courses in Engineering are issued by respective RECs.

Allocation of seats : Each REC has reserved 50% seats for the students of the state in which it is located and the rest 50% seats are for students belonging to other states, according to quota fixed for each state on the basis of its population.

Selection of the candidates is made on the basis of merit ;of the Combined Pre entrance Test (CPET) which is conducted by one of the RECs

You may obtain the application form from the Principal of the REC, having jurisdiction over the area from which you have passed the qualifying exam. One form is sufficient for applying for admission to the Regional Engineering College of your area under reserved quota seats and for admission to the other RECs in the country against seats meant for candidates from outside states except in case RECs where admission forms are required to be filled in coursewise.
