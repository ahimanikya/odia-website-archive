Nandighosha visits Orissa 

Nandighosha is the chariot that carries Lord Jagannath. Devotees from all faith pull it. We at Nandighosha.Org are committed to provide the chariot carrying the SUCCESS of Orissa. We are striving hard to make it the e-vehicle for Orissa's true success in education, information exchanging and adoption to the latest technology. 

The primary resource of Nandighosha is its Bahakas (volunteers), their talent, creativity and willingness to commit to goals that catch their imagination and quench their thirst to do something constructive. We love being part of a dynamic and challenging environment. We start by providing a framework from which we can define the culture of an organization and create an organization that is fostering education and learning on a continuous basis. 

We need your support as Bahakas and Bahikas: 

Two of our Bahakas are traveling to Orissa for face-to-face meetings with all of you who are interested to make Orissa a success through our Nandighosha efforts. At present we are limiting our seminars to Bhubaneswar and Berhampur. With your encouragement we will extend our seminars to other places such as Sambalpur, Balasore, and Rourkela in the future. Since, Nandighosha is an unique concept in cyberspace, we thought it would be a good venture to get your first hand input about what you would desire from us as service and how we could rely on your involvement.

Bahaka Sunil Sabat is a Silicon Valley veteran of 11 years, who has worked and consulted with companies like Intel, I2, Cadence and Charles Schwab.

Bahaka Subhranshu Das has a MCA and MBA to his credit with 15 years of total IT experience across the globe. He started his career with Govt. of Orissa and then moved to private sector in India, Japan, Canada, UK before finally landing in USA. At present he is working in USA for the last five years.

The following schedule when Bahaka Sabat and Bahaka Das will be available for one-on-one discussions with you. On behalf of Nandighosha we welcome you to the meetings and hope we can provide you with satisfactory service after we receive your feedback. We look forward to your attendance in large numbers and hope to see you amongst us as a Bahaka.
