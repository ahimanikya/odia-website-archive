Joining Nandighosha Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What are the requirements for joining Nandighosha and am I eligible to join?

A: Nandighosha membership is open to virtually anyone with an interest in engineering or computer science. So feel free to apply. The grade assigned to new members will vary depending on academic accomplishment and work experience. 

Technical and non-technical applicants who would benefit by membership and participation in the Nandighosha enter as Associate members.  Member and Senior Member grades recognize those who have achieved professional proficiency, as demonstrated by degrees received and/or work experience. 

Membership dues are the same for all members (except Students who pay substantially reduced membership dues) although Regional assessments vary depending upon where you live.  Funds collected as Region assessments are returned to local entities to support meetings, newsletters and other Region-based member activities.   All members receive the same benefits and services with the exception of voting privileges and holding elected office.  These benefits are limited to those holding Member grade or higher.

Note : At this point of time all Membership is free

Q: How do I get a membership application?

A: You may join Nandighosha online  at http://www.nandighosha.org/forum/policy.asp.  To request a membership application by postal mail, e-mail your name, address and the type of application you need  to info@nandighosha.org.

Q: Do I have to make payment before my membership is active?

A: Membership is free for all at this point of time.

Q: What are the benefits of joining Nandighosha?

A: For an overview of some of the key membership benefits, visit http://www.nandighosha.org/home/services.asp

Q. What should I expect when I apply for Nandighosha membership

A: An Acknowledgment letter sent within 4 business days of receipt of the application.  Acknowledgment letters are sent via email. 

If you have further question and concern please feel free to drop us an email at info@nandighosha.org
