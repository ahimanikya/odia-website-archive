Services of Nandighosha:

Nandighosha, the e-wheel of Orissa will provide Information Technology related services to training institutes, corporate, educational institutes, both government and non-government organizations and individuals. We, the Bahakas, at Nandighosha aim to promote IT awareness in Orissa for which we would be organizing 

        - IT Mela’s
        - Seminars and workshops
        - Question and Answer sessions
        - Web site evaluation.
        - Will take up project assessment 
        - Prototypes.
        - 24/7 Online professional advice.

We include in some of our services minor fees to meet certain expenses during organizing. Please check out the kind of service to know more detail about it.

IT Mela’s: Nandighosha along with a parent organization in Orissa (to be selected from the one’s who have applied) would organize IT Mela. Nandighosha will try to bring in expert organization and people to provide the cutting edge. Fees will be charged from the participating organizations and those who wish to display their products. A detail work-plan will be carried out with the partner organization.

IT Mela’s would include seminars and workshops, but this can be arranged separately too. Nandighosha will select a training or educational institute to organize seminars and workshop on topics of mutual interest. We would aim to involve more of students and expose them to the latest trends in IT and latest development.

Question and Answer sessions: Apart from the IT mela’s and seminars where Q&A would be an integral part, Nandighosha will also provide “FREE” online consultancy to individuals through its discussion forum and online chat options. Our experts (Bahakas) will login at specified time, which is to be announced previously, and would try to answer your queries and provide a real time online solution. Any such query that needs a wider involvement of people will be archived by Nandighosha and the concerned person will be intimated its status within 2 working days (48 hrs).

Web site evaluation: The vast experience and expertise of Bahakas would enable them to evaluate web site in terms of its content, design/development and functionality. Please submit your web site now and get it evaluated. We would rate it and enter it into our monthly contest of “best web site award”.

How viable is your project? Nandighosha and its Bahakas will help you assess your project and assist you give it the right shape. We charge a nominal fee for taking up project assessment and evaluation. If you feel that our expertise can be of your help, then don’t hesitate to contact us.

Prototypes and professional advise (24/7 Online Service): We look forward to your assistance and association to achieve this. In case you are an institute, entrepreneur, organization that is in need of this cutting edge or plan to associate with us then send in your requirements and details using the feedback form. If you wish to associate yourself or your organization with us then join in from our membership page after checking the terms and conditions page.
