About Nandighosha

Nandighosha is an initiative to promote IT awareness in Orissa, started by a group of highly motivated and selfless people. Nandighosha strives to promote the development of Information Technology and allied sciences, in the state of Orissa, for the advancement of the profession and the well being of its members and people. Nandighosha is open to all individuals who are interested to promote IT awareness in state of Orissa. The ultimate aim is to help Orissa emerge as the IT-superpower of India and the World. Lets join hands together to move this chariot in the direction of development and prosperity.

Mission of Nandighosha:

Nandighosha is a fast growing organization formed by a group of enthusiasts with the vision of fostering eCulture in the state of Orissa. We as an organization are passionate to help Orissa attain the cutting edge effectiveness that will make it excel in the present aggressive eWorld. One way to accomplish this goal is to reach out to people who have the potential and the desire to distinguish themselves and need a helping hand. 

Starting with information technology as initial sector of focus, and educational institutes as core members of our organization, we plan to extend our service to other areas of technology and education with a view to include businesses and government in future. We intend to be the information warehouse for training, jobs, businesses, partners and all that matters in our daily lives. By including both you and your stakeholders on the same platform, we are enabling mass scale instant information exchange and dissemination. 

We are non-profit and result oriented organization with a mission to provide Proper Avenue for exchange of vital information so that nobody is deprived of his or her goals for the lack of such information.
Lets join hands to make this dream a reality and accelerate IT awareness in Orissa !

Values:

As an Organization we strive to develop trust and pride in the organization and in the work we do.

Professional Integrity
Conducting all activities with integrity, professionalism and commitment.

Openness
Being open to new ideas and maintaining an open organization. 

Learning
Creating a learning organization in which members seek and share knowledge and information, and are committed to personal and common growth.

Innovation
Striving to address training needs for high-quality service and seeking better ways to achieve our goals.

Team Work
Co-operating with one another and our partners in education in an open and supportive way to achieve shared goals.

Excellence
Maintaining high standards of performance and professionalism, and aiming for excellence in everything we do, including being fair, ethical and trustworthy in all we do.
