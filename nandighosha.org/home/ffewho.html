Who qualifies for scholarship?

There are four major criteria in selecting the candidates

a) The student has to be extraordinarily bright.
b) The student has to be financially underprivileged
c) The student should be in an approved field of study.
d) The student should have a Facilitator in India and a Coordinator in USA.

A) Academic excellence

In general, excellence is defined in terms of superior achievement with respect to the peer group of the student and generally accepted norms of excellence in a particular field of education.  The foundation relies heavily on the judgement of it's facilitators to the definition of excellence.   The following are the guidelines for selection.

High School level: Above 70% marks in the last three consecutive years is usually a good indicator of excellence. 
Medical/college:  A student will meet the criteria of excellence if he/she secures 55% marks in addition to a good 3 year track record of over 70% marks.
Engineering/B.A/B.SC Degree:  A student has to score marks in excess of 60% in all previous university examinations in addition to a good 3 year track record in high school of above 70% marks.

B) Financial Qualification

The student is considered financially underprivileged if the family circumstances are such that it cannot take the burden of sending the student to school or college.  The selection committee will take into consideration the family income, family assets and family expense.  Family expense might include expense due to more than one child in college or unusual medical expenditure for a family member.

Each case is judged on its individual merit.  The general principle being that the foundation's assistance is available to those students who are able to establish a case that their continued pursuit of education is at risk without outside help.  The student has to demonstrate to the foundation that the combined resources of family and other sources of assistance, such as, government scholarships are not enough to support continuation of education.  Here are some rules of thumb.

  • If the head of the family is a class I or II officer in central or state government or any public sector undertaking,  it will be hard for the student to make a case that he/she will not be continuing education except for help from the foundation.

  • In case of private sector employment, if the monthly family income exceeds Rs 8000.00 significant other evidence will be needed to persuade the foundation that the student deserves its assistance.

  • If the combined assets of the family include significant real estate holdings, jewellery or other valuable assets it will be a difficult case to argue for assistance.

C) Approved field of study

The foundations support is available for post-primary school education from class VIII all the way to graduate education leading to B.A., B.Sc., M.B.B.S., B.Tech., degrees etc.   It is not available for post graduate studies in professional fields or for education outside india.  In general, the foundation's assistance is meant for excellence in education in the traditional sense.  The foundation is not supporting pursuit of excellence in other fields such as athletics, fine arts and performing arts.

D) The Scholarship Amount
The scholarship will cover direct educational expenses, such as fees, books, equipment and other expenses connected with schooling. In some cases expenses on boarding and lodging may also be considered.   The scholarship amount depends on the need of the student.  The need will be established by how much money is needed for fees, books etc. along with family financial conditions.  Financial aid can vary from Rs.five hundred per year for a high school to several thousand rupees per year for a medical student.  If the student already has some financial help,  only the required difference will be provided.  If all the data cannot be provided in the application,  it is recommended that the applicant write a detailed letter describing the family circumstances etc.

E) Duration of the Scholarship
The scholarships are granted for one academic year and can be renewed for subsequent years provided the student maintains academic excellence.  The renewal will end at the end of the curriculum e.g. a high school student can renew his scholarship until the end of high school.  He or she will have to re-apply for a college scholarship.  The responsibility of initiating renewal of the scholarship is with the student.  The foundation will not automatically renew the scholarship.  Details of renewal procedures are given in the later paragraphs.

Selection Process:
The eligible students are usually identified by a Facilitator in India and are jointly sponsered by him and a Coordinator based in the USA.  The decision to grant scholarship is not solely based on certificates but also on the recommendation of the Facilitator/Coordinator.   Unless sponsored by a Facilitator and recommended by a Coordinator scholarship applications are not entertained.

An application has to be made on the prescribed form and sent to the Foundation through the Facilitator and the US Coordinator. Application forms are not directly sent to students. A Facilitator gives out the application upon satisfying himself that the student would meet the eligibility requirements.

The functions of a Facilitator are:
A. The Facilitator knows the student personally and can vouch for the financial need of the student.
B. The Facilitator will continue to be in contact with the student and guide him/her if necessary until the end of the education.
C. The facilitator will send a Monitoring report while forwarding applications for renewal of scholarship at the commencement of next year.
D. The facilitator will oversee the expenditure of the student.

The functions of the Coordinator are:
The Coordinator is a volunteer who necessarily resides in the USA and is responsible to the foundation.  The functions of the Coordinator are as follows:

A. Selects and establishes Facilitators in India.
B. Has full confidence in the integrity and dedication of the Facilitator.
C. Vouches that the Facilitator is capable of guiding the student.
D. Obtains additional information etc. if needed for evaluation process.
E. Finds another Facilitator for the student if the previous one withdraws as a Facilitator.

Evaluation process
Members of the 'Scholarship Review Committee'generally meet twice a month to review all pending applications. The committee reviews all the applications and decides either to approve or reject the application.  

Renewal Procedure
At the end of one year, if the student decides to apply for renewal of the scholarship he/she can do so by filling out the prescribed Renewal Application Form.  After obtaining the Facilitator's comments on it, it should be sent to the US Coordinator who   in turn  would forward it to the FFE office with his own remarks.  A renewal application form is usually sent along with the initial check for the scholarship amount.
