Job Title : Network Engineer I Company : Nuclear Management Company (NMC) Location : Hudson, WI Responsibilities : Develop, plan and implement the overall strategic goals of the company's WAN. The Network Engineer also is responsible for and participates in all WAN technical services functions in design, engineering, and architecture planning for the organization's future networking needs. Directs and manages third party services and vendors needed to support the WAN. This position also provides second level support for Network operational issues. Requirements : Candidates must have knowledge of Cisco and Bay/Nortel routers, and LAN switches. Must possess a good hands on, methodical ability to solve problems. Understanding of Network monitoring tools, such as CiscoWorks , HP Openview and RMON probes. LAN/WAN Troubleshooting - IOS, Hardware, Frame-Relay, ATM WAN, Gigabit Ethernet. Process oriented with ability to follow change management procedures. Maintaining and Updating Network Documentation. Familiar with network security procedures and Checkpoint Firewall management. Maintaining and updating Checkpoint Firewalls (Solaris and Nokia). Working Knowledge of the following protocols and technologies: Fast Ethernet, Gigabit Ethernet,802.1p/Q VLANs, IP, Routing, VPN, EIGRP, OSPF, PPP, Frame-Relay, ATM WAN, SNMP. A Bachelor's degree in Networking or Data communications is desired with 3+ years of experience in TCP/IP, frame relay and Checkpoint Firewalls preferred. Microsoft certifications are a plus. <b><i>E-mail resume to</i> : jobs@nmcco.com</b> for immediate consideration.
