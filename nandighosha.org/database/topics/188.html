Orissa Infotech News The Government of Orissa has finalised a scheme in Dec. 2000 to introduce 5 year Computer Education course in all High Schools from class 6 to 10 th with the help of private computer education companies. According to the order these companies would start the classes with help of franchisees in the schools and collect the cost from the students. The companies have been alloted the districts as under: Edutech Informatics- Puri, Ganjam,Phulbani,Koraput &Kalahandi Aptech Ltd- Cuttack,Balasore,Keonjhar&Mayurbhanj. L C C Ltd- Dhenkanal,Sundargarh,Sambalpur& Bolangir. The entrepreneurs engaged as franchisies would deploy 4 computers,printers etc and engage faculty for theory and practical. They would also pay the company Startup fee,Security deposit& monthly Royalty. they would collect maximum of Rs45 per month from students joining it. The courses of studies has been prepared by Orissa Computer Application Center along with the companies. Secretary Education Ashoke Tripathy is taking great interest. Already centers have opened in Collegiate School, ParryMohan Academy at Cuttack and Anugul High School. Many schools are in pipe line. There is enthusiasm among parents to teach children computer. It is a good initiative from the cash strapped Government. However the scheme looks economically viable in towns and large schools with more than 500 students. As most of the Rural schools have about 250 students it is unlikely that any body needing an invesement of Rs 2 lakhs would rush to take the franchise. Digital Divide likely to persist. Results would be visible by Jan. 2002. This innovative initiative deserves all good wishes. <font size=5></font id=size5><font size=6></font id=size6>
