VLSI is Very Large Scale Integration. It is technology of designing and manufacturing complex IC ( Integrated Circuits). IC development has taken place in two main streams, biplolar and MOS ( Metal Oxide Semiconductor) technologies. At the outset, bipolar technology seemed to provide the most promising approach to develop logic arrays, but MOS technology has come thru with huge success in high scale integration and speed. Sunil Sabat
