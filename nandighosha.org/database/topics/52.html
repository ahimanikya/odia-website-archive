<h3>Quality Assurance is a very hot career in US.</h3> In India there is not much of an emphasis on this aspect of software engineering due to lack of quality focus which in turn is due to lack of any major software <b>product</b> development in India. The situation in US is very different due to the abundance of software products that are developed in this market. The demand for QA professionals is very high and the supply is comparatively low. Hence, this is a very attrative career opportunity often commanding comparatively higher salaries and perks. Experience is valued very highly in the QA field just like it is in the fields of Database administration and application design. This is due to the fact that it is very difficult to learn the skills in these fields and the issues that you learn to tackle never become outdated. Hence the skills that you develop are relevant and applicable to a wide range of situations. In conclusion: In todays is market in the United States QA is one of the hottest carrer options all you need is the right attitude and the skill to do the job right.
