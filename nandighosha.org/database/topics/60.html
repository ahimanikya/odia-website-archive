What appserver you are using? J2EE is a little wider technology to go on first. I would suggest you to learn first ejb then eventually you would be deploying enterprise beans in J2EE environment. Here is the my simple answer for what is EJB?Enterprise JavaBean is nothing but a specification of how to develope distributed components and help develpoers to concentrate on business logic leaving other worries like Transaction Management,Security Control ... to EJB Container(don't worry what is container rightnow you can understand as AppServer). What you need to develope and deploy an Enterprise Bean? 1.Implement the bean 2.Specify what type of bean and other bean and environment properties. 3.Tell the container about your new bean and what are the responsibilities he should do for you :) 4. Here you end up in creating a partlly(full) distributed component. 5. Let any clients to use your component. I told you a big story in very few lines. We can go forward by you asking the questions and myself or anybody can give answers. Hope this will give you a way to start. Asha Balasubramanyan http://www.ammu.org
