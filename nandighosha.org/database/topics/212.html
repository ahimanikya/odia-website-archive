IFC Joins Hands With LCC To Introduce I T In Orissa. A news item datelined Calcutta i.e. Kolkata in Economic Times on 26th June reports that International Finance Corporation has done a survey in Orissa to fund LCC’s venture to introduce Computer Education in Orissan schools. They are funding Rs 25 crores for setting up Computer Centers in 1028 schools. By a December 2000 order the Orissa Government had given permission to 3 private Companies to set up Computer Education Classes in all districts of Orissa. LCC had been earmarked undivided Dhenkanal,Sundargarh Sambalpur and Bolangir Districts. They were to start computer classes in schools through their franchisees on collection of Rs 45 pm from the students..The subject would be taught from VIth to Xth classes. Courses of studies has been prepared by OCAC in consultation with these companies. Last known Aptech has started classes in 40 schools in its area. Please see full report in the annexure.
