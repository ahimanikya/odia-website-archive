Arun Patnaik · 2001-06-29 11:19:26

Hi Sunil, The nature of IT is such that the technology landscape is very dynamic. However usually the life cycle of each technology is about 5 years in the minimum so any new technology that comes in will be usually applicable in some context for atleast 5 years. The dynamic nature of the technology trends is especially problematic for junior engineers since those are the jobs that are very dependent on the CURRENT HOT technology. My suggestions are as follows: 1. Don't worry about the changing landscape pick a substantial technology and master in that. For example Java/C++ if you are a master at either you won't have trouble finding s job. Note that you have to cover these technologies well i.e. for Java you need to understand J2EE and other advanced Java technologies. 2. Always stay on top of emerging trends in your area of expertise. For example, if you are working on Java you need to be aware of emerging trends in this area like SOAP, XML, MessageBeans etc. 3. Work on stable technology, like Oracle or C but master this really well. There is still high demand for advanced C programmers or advanced SQL programmers/Database admins. Finally the key is to master in a substantial technology covering wide enough ground in the corresponding field. Thanks - Arun Arun Patnaik Staff S/w Engineer II Sybase Inc
