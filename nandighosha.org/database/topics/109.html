Qualitative words are used to convey the same meaning but vary in quality of use. For example: Excellent, Fabulous, Outstanding, and Fantastic All four of the above words could be used to convey almost the same meaning. Your choice is Excellent Your choice is Fabulous Your choice is Outstanding Your choice is Fantastic Now, all four words could be used alternately to describe your choice. Let me ask this question: Which word describes your choice as Supreme or Best? Please post your reply. Debasmita Misra Senior Engineer Kewaunee Nuclear Power Plant Kewaunee, WI 54115
