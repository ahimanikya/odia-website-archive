<b>Job Title</b> Information Management Analyst <b>Location</b> Kewaunee, WI, USA <b>Responsibilities</b> Candidate must analyze, evaluate and implement software applications that meet the NUCCO business plan. Provide overall coordination for consistent, efficient use of the CMMS Work Management software (EMPAC) and Electronic Document Management software (Curator) within the Nuclear Group. Reports to the Manager - Information Management. Direct and coach Nuclear employees in the utilization of software products and applications: <li>administer day to day process facilitation and direct support for all functional aspects of CMMS, including establishing and maintaining security levels</li> <li>analyze and program report applications identified by the end users</li> <li>collaborates with all Nuclear groups to develop an understanding of the process improvement opportunities and process changes necessary to fully utilize the CMMS software.</li> Provide information management business consulting and perform research: <li>investigate, evaluate, and recommend technologies and techniques for development, maintenance, and enhancement of business solutions for the Nuclear group</li> <li>evaluate alternatives to address business requirements and translates requirements into solutions for small projects or larger projects with general guidance</li> <li> analyze business requirements to create and maintain physical databases and database applications.</li> <li>obtain and document business requirements, prepare user acceptance test scripts, test applications</li> <li>investigate and resolve CMMS transaction interface errors that occurred between DB2, Sybase and Oracle using SQL Navigator and Desktop DBA software.</li> Organizes, prepares, and conducts training of software applications utilized within the department: <li>first line contact within the department of information technology problem identification and resolutions</li> <li>provide link between end user and the information technology personnel to meet process requirements</li> <li>review and ensure Information Management requirements for system are established and maintained.</li> <b><u>Emergency Functions</u></b> Perform emergency plan duties: <li>maintain knowledge of overall emergency plan</li> <li>respond to emergency drills and carry out duties as assigned such as assembly area coordinator and/or support activities.</li> <b>Requirements</b> Candidates must possess a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Management or Business preferred. Associated Degree plus 5 years experience or 10 years experience in related work activity acceptable. Extensive experience in EMPAC, Curator, and SQL Navigator. Moderate experience in computer technology. Some experience in Warehouse Management System (WMS) to support CMMS interfaces. Postion proficiency of 1-2 years. Knowledge of basic principles of accounting. Working knowledge of information systems, DB2, Sybase, and Oracle. Knowledge of MMS, MCS, and WMA applications to support CMMS interfaces. Understanding nuclear processes and technical support requirements. Knowledge of network and software configurations. Must satisfactorily complete required exams. Knowledge of Quality Assurance program, American National Standards Institute N45.2.9, Administrative Control Directive 9.2, 10 CFR 50 Appendix B Criterion 17, and section 6 of Technical Specifications. Usual workweek consists of five eight-hour days. When working outage hours, job may involve six ten-hour days. Job duties generally performed in an indoor office environment, and may involve exposure to hazards normally associated with Nuclear Power Plant. <b><i>e-mail resume to</i></b> jobs@nmcco.com
