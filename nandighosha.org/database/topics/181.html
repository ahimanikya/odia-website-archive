Cisco Networking Academy in Orissa New Delhi, May 14: Cisco Systems, a world leader in networking for the Internet, will set up their Networking Academy Programme in Orissa enabling it to establish Local Networking Academies in engineering colleges in the State. A memorandum of understanding to this effect was signed today between S P Nanda, secretary, IT, Orissa government, and chairman Orissa Computer Application Centre (OCAC), and Manoj Chugh, president, India and SAARC Region, Cisco Systems. OCAC will be designated as a Regional Networking Academy and receive networking equipment, comprehensive training, the Networking Academy curriculum and technical support from Cisco Systems. Cisco will also implement the Cisco Networking Academy quality assurance plan to help maintain the highest quality of training and education at these institutions. The academy programme focusses on teaching students how to design, build, and maintain computer networks. Chugh said this was another step in the Cisco commitment to establish 34 Cisco Regional Networking Academies in every state and Union Territory in India.
