When Criticizing Others... <ul> <li>Make your comments specific. <ul> <li>Attempt to provide the person with some valuable information.</li> <li>Help them to understand exactly what needs to change.</li> </ul> </li> <li>Be sure the criticized behavior can be changed. <ul> <li>If the person can do nothing about the problem, you will probably just make things worse by being critical of it.</li> </ul> </li> <li>Use assertive communication. <ul> <li>Speak calmly and try not to let your emotions dictate the conversation.</li> <li>Try not to shame, humiliate, or blame the person.</li> </ul> </li> <li>Give the person a reason to change. <ul> <li>Inform them of any benefits which might come out of acting on your suggestions.</li> </ul> </li> <li>Time your criticisms well. <ul> <li>Avoid criticizing someone in public.</li> <li>Wait until the person is in a reasonably good mood.</li> </ul> </li> <li>View constructive criticism as feedback not punishment. <ul> <li>Positive change should be your goal.</li> </ul> </li> </ul> Debasmita Misra Senior Engineer (Training & Simulation) Kewaunee Nuclear Power Plant Kewaunee, WI 54115
