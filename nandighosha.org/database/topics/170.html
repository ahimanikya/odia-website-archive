<b><font color=purple>Elaborated Opinion Statements</font id=purple></b> <li>Begin with a personal pronoun: "I think that..."; "My opinion is..."</li> <li>Use a compound sentence containing several phrases connected by such words as because, therefore, and but: "I disagree with what you've said because..." or "I agree with your first point, but..."</li> <li>You do not need to have an original argument in order to express your opinion. You may rephrase, repeat, or comment on what another person has said.</li> <li>You may agree or disagree with what others say. Or you may change the direction of the conversation: "I think we're ignoring an important point, which is..."</li> <b><font color=purple>Breaking into an Ongoing Conversation</font id=purple></b> <li>Listen actively -- nod, look directly at others, say "uh-huh."</li> <li>Wait for a natural pause in the conversation.</li> <li>Raise your voice slightly to signal others you wish to speak.</li> <li>Use your body -- lean forward into the conversational arena; use hand gestures; touch the person to whom you wish to speak.</li> <li>State an opinion, "I think that..." or ask a question, "What about..."</li> <li>Use the person's name to gain attention, "Bill, I also think..."</li> <li>"Excuse me, may I join you?"</li> <li>"I don't know exactly what you're talking about, but it sounds fascinating."</li> <b><font color=purple>Resisting Interruption</font id=purple></b> <li>Raise your voice slightly to signal that you would like to finish your comment.</li> <li>Repeat your opening phrase so that you don't lose your train of thought, "I think...but I do think that..."</li> <li>Continue talking without hesitation; engage in parallel talking for a short while.</li> <li>Don't look at the interrupter; look at those who are attentive.</li> <li>Ask the interrupter to wait until you have finished your statement, "I think the best thing to do would... please wait a minute... would be to start a new program."</li> <li>Hold up your hand or touch the person to signal that you would like the interrupter to stop.</li> <li>Pause briefly, then quickly resume your comment, "I think that... the new program idea is a good one."</li> <li>If interruption is a question, briefly reply and resume comment.</li> <li>"I'll be back to that in a minute."</li> Debasmita Misra Senior Engineer (Training & Simulation) Kewaunee Nuclear Power Plant Kewaunee, WI 54115
