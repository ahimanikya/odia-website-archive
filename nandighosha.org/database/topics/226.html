Bellow is a job description. Is this something you or someone you know would be interested in. Please check it out and let me know. Under general direction: Designs, analyzes, evaluates, codes, tests, debugs, and implements complex software applications in support of various functional areas. May provide support for production applications. Formulates/defines system scope and objectives, considering computer equipment capacity and limitations, operating time, and form of desired results. Prepares detailed specifications from which complex programs will be written. Works independently at a highly technical level through all phases of applications systems analysis and programming. Exercises judgment within generally defined practices and policies in selecting methods and techniques for obtaining solutions. Performs and/or oversees others in project management activities, such as estimating, scheduling, and monitoring tasks for moderately sized projects from initiation through completion. Regularly provides guidance and training to less experienced analysts/programmers. May assist internal customers with questions regarding the functionality of applications. May work with vendors to research and resolve significant issues with existing packaged software, as well as make recommendations for future enhancements. May assist with implementation and/or integration of packaged software systems. Provides necessary project and product documentation, forms, training, and testing to support projects and for customer acceptance of systems and processes. May manage contract system professionals. Must have a solid technical foundation, real world experience architecting, designing and developing large commercial software applications, and the ability to technically supervise and guide junior team members. Qualified candidate must have 5 years of Software Engineering exp. in the following areas: -Object oriented analysis, design and programming real world exp. -Understanding of design patterns and their application -Distributed computing -Java 2 platform software development exp., must understand RMI, JDBC, servlets, JSP, EJBs, J2ME, JNDI, LDAP, security, HTTP, HTML. XML, WAP -Real world exp. develping Web applications, internet portals and wireless internet systems - JAIN (Java API for integrated networks), OSA (open services architecture), SS7, H.323, SIP (session initiation protocol), GPRS, 2.5G standards and concepts and characteristics of circuit-switched and packet-switched networks. Must display a firm understanding of distributed system architectures, with exp. designing and developing commercial distributred system software. The candidate must also be able to formulate and guide system integration efforts, and understand how to design large subsystems for integration, with a focused understanding of the full software development life cycle, including software testing and its impact on architecture, design and development. The qualified candidate must also be familiar with process management concepts, software development methodologies, and in particular, be fluent with RUP and be able to guide or mentor a team of software engineers in properly applying the RUP methodology in the creation of a large-scale real-world mobile internet system. The ideal candidate should have a background in wireless telecommunications, and understanding the challenges to creating a wireless internet portal. 10% to 15% international travel is required.
