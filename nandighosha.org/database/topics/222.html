<b>Job Title</b> Engineer/Sr. Engineer - I/T Computers <b>Location</b> Prairie Island, MN (Company: Nuclear Management Company) <b>Responsibilities</b> The successful applicant will participate in the development of software and hardware solutions for the replacement/upgrade of the Emergency Response Computer System (ERCS) as well as the continued maintenance of that system. ERCS system duties include programming to develop new or revised applications, develop new or revised displays, point I/O database maintenance, OSI Plant Information (PI) subsystem support, participate in rotating primary contact call list for off-hours support, support of ERCS simulator unique functionality, diagnosing problems as they arise, suggesting solutions to prevent reoccurrence and development of new strategies to connect input data from new sources to the ERCS. All hardware/software changes are per plant procedures and guidelines. Assignments in other process computer systems such as security, training simulator, and others are possible. Essential functions include: <li>Participate in design teams for changes, modifications, and general maintenance of the ERCS.</li> <li>Provide support to ERCS users via telephone, email, and/or in person.</li> <li>Complete/coordinate reviews of ERCS functions and software.</li> <li>Attend training required by the Institute of Nuclear Power Operation Accredited training program.</li> <li>Participate in the Prairie Island Emergency Response Organization.</li> <li>Work with other plant organizations in maintenance of ERCS equipment and functions.</li> <b>Requirements</b> Candidates must possess a BS degree from an accredited institution in engineering or computer science in a discipline related to process computing systems or equivalent. Experience in FORTRAN and C++ is very desirable. Knowledge in OpenVMS, Visual Basic, DataViews DV-Draw, relational database management, network communication protocols such as TCP/IP, web- based application programming and web page design is helpful. Must have strong documentation and problem solving skills. Previous experience in the nuclear industry is preferred. Previous experience with process monitoring systems is desirable. Some travel with possible extended stays may be required. This position is nuclear sensitive and the candidate must pass the Nuclear Management Company’s nuclear screening requirements, including a physical examination, drug test, and security background check. Selection criteria may include testing and/or assessment. Pre-employment screening is required. <b><i>send resume to:</i></b> jobs@nmcco.com
