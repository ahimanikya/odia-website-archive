<center><b><u>AN APPEAL TO ALL ODIYAS</u></b></center> As you may be aware that some people have already been affected by the severe downturn of economy and potentially this situation might deteriorate further. It can be a very harrowing experience - especially in a land where one does not have many acquaintances. As per the recent news, a number of major companies have announced job cut in the past few months, refer this URL for a detail list http://dailynews.yahoo.com/h/ap/20010420/bs/job_cuts_glance_1.html And we know many people from our community also lost their job, looking for job and planning to go back to India. I think it's the right time where we all need to help our own people who are in need At Nandighosha.Org I have started two forums: Looking for job - people can post their resume if they are looking for job here. To post your Resume visit: http://www.nandighosha.org/forum/forum.asp?FORUM_ID=26&CAT_ID=8&Forum_Title=Looking+for+Job+%3F How to upload your Resume? Click on the above link. Click on new Topic. Write a brief note about what you are looking for. Then Click on the "Attach File" clip to upload your resume. You can register to avoid overriding, or just rename the file locally before uploading so that it will unique, e.g. Ahimanikya_Resume.doc. I have created an account in case if you don't want to register here you can use this [Historical shared login detail omitted] [Historical shared login detail omitted] Job Announcements- people can post job announcements, if you have a job opening in your company, please post the details. Also, if you could help someone who has posted his/her resume, please send it to the appropriate department in your company so that the person gets considered for the job. To post announcements Visit: http://www.nandighosha.org/forum/forum.asp?FORUM_ID=28&CAT_ID=8&Forum_Title=Job+Announcements If you have suggestions on their resume or you want to give some interview tips, call or email to jobseekers. That way we could find out their experience and put in a word wherever possible. Please act now without any delay. Please spread the word in other Odiya mailing lists. Please forward this mail to other odiyas you know and not in this mailing list. Regards Nandighosha Team <b><u>About Nandighosha:</b></u> This is an Initiative to promote IT awareness in Orissa started in late 2000 by few Bay Area Software professional lead by Sunil Sabat, CA.
