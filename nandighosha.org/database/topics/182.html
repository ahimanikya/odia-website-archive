From: "Purna Mohanty" <mohanty_calnet@hotmail.com> Date: Wed May 16, 2001 8:19 am Subject: FFE's Contribution to Orissa FFE has supported 24 (0.9% of Total) students in Orissa with a disbursement of Rs. 4,64,050 during Academic Year 2000-2001. It is a 1200% increase from 1999-2000, when it supported just 2 students. But it is not close to the number of students being supported in other states. In TN, 1127 (44.5%) students got support with Rs.10,180,587. In West Bengal, 185 (7.3%) students got support with Rs.1,200,968. In Maharasthra, 359 (14.2%) students got support with Rs.4,441,169. In Karnataka, 256 (10.1%) students got support with Rs.2,335,387. Attached is the doc, which has all the breakups. Orissa is not even close. The reason being we need to find deserving candidates (poor and brilliant studnets) in Orissa. With minimal effort on your part, you can make a big difference in the life of the poor and brilliant student in particular, and contribute to your state in general by bringing out the inner strength of the student (which, otherwise, might have been lost for ever without your help). Please visit http://www.ffe.org and help getting more candidates from Orissa. With best regards, Purna
