Job Title: Network Coordinator (4 positions) Location: Kewaunee Nuclear Power Plant Company: Nuclear Management Company (NMC) (http://www.nmc-rs.com) Responsibilities: Responsible for the operation, maintenance, and upgrades to the KNPP computer network. This is a dynamic opportunity for an individual with prior information technology experience to support the existing Novell 5.1 / Windows 95 environment, then assist with the migration to a Windows 2000 client and server environment in 2002. Duties include network monitoring, working with departments to determine department requirements, 1st and 2nd level technical support, and working with clients, vendors, and other technical staff members to resolve issues. One year of the work experience must be in the customer service area.
