Latest on Sending of Computers to Schools in India. Ther is a good news for those philanthropically minded Or emotionally attached to their alma mater. The government of India had issued a notification on 11.2.1999 Exempting Used Computers ,printers,scanners,monitor etc.,from all Customs duties when it was donated from abroad to any govt. school run by Central, State Govt.s or Local bodies. The Govt. of India has now issued a Notification on 29.05.2001 that computers can now be donated to any school , i.e private schools, Church run schools or society run schools and the same would cleared free of duty from port, air port etc. The condition attaced is that such private schools shall produce a certificate from any state authorities that they are recognised schools and that it is run on a non commercial basis. At the time of clearance of the goods from the customs a certificate is to be produced under the signature of the Head master about the above position. In case of govt schools a certficate from another govt. body (may be panchayat) that it is a govt school is better. This new Notn. # is 57/2001 -cus dated 29.05.2001 .
