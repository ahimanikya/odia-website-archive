Ball and chain can be humourous or insulting. It refers to one's wife. *Ball and chain refers to prisoners that were shakled by a chain to a heavy metal ball so they could not escape. Harry told the guys at the office that he could not stay at the party too late because his ball and chain was waiting for him at home. Mary K. St. Germain
