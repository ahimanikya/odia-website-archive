<font color=pink></font id=pink> Software development projects are accomplished in a group .As developing software needs a very strong analytical mind and marshalling of knowledge, we can collect more information from various people before proceeding. Feasibility study, analysis, design, coding, testing, implementation and maintenance are done as per the various models meant to execute it. Group effort during feasibility study and analysis is indispensable . During feasibility study to start projects brings several alternative solutions. We summarize the best solution for a project as per the technical, operational and economic feasibility. But effective time management and effort estimation to complete the project is sometimes bit time consuming if we decide the same in a group .It is an Herculean task for the software maker to decide for a formal method .Once a new method is determined , everybody in the project must be educated in the new methods . So moving to an established culture from an informal method of doing the same thing takes time, determination and a good cost benefit ratio. It amounts to a cultural change and introducing culture change is risky business. Once the new methods are established, it still takes a continuing commitment from the management to keep them alive and in use. Somehow Science and arts are mutually exclusive. Scope of work of engineers and scientists need not require much creativity and artists should not be educated to be competent in measurement or science as it may hurt their creativity. Software development requires a balance between creativity and methods. Here software software engineering approach comes into picture . Though standard processes are already in existence , each software project is unique and we have to customize the standard processes as per the project . This customization of processes followed in a group of experiences project managers. Once upon a time a philosopher remarked "Only one thing is permanent in the world i.e change ". Change is inevitable in Software projects. Even if a rigorous group analysis is done , change management is a continuos process and is applied to all the stages of SDLC . Defect estimation ,preventaion and detection are quite vital .Several defect estimation tools are available currently in the market to accomplish the same . It may so happen that a defect is detected at last stage of the SDLC cycle which is unknowingly injected during first stage . Code walk through and group review by experienced people decreases the defects . Defect logs are maintained and errors are subsequently removed as per the severity .Improvement in quality and productivity needs good people ,processes and technology . The most effective solution will normally be a blend of several approaches . The challenge is to achieve the correct balance creative freedom and engineering discipline .The science of engineering provides methods that are a good fit for the needs of software development and testing .However care must be taken that these methods do not become rigid and unchangeable or they will cease to be a benefit . Solution always lies with people not technology .Any technology will does not take human behaviour into account is apt to fail .
