Keynote Session - Summer 2001

administrator · 2001-07-02 00:00:00

Two of our Bahakas are travelling to Orissa for face-to-face meetings with all of you who are interested to make Orissa a success through our Nandighosha efforts. At present we are limiting our seminars to Bhubaneswar and Berhampur. With your encouragement we will extend our seminars to other places such as Sambalpur, Balasore, and Rourkela in the future. Since, Nandighosha is an unique concept in cyberspace, we thought it would be a good venture to get your first hand input about what you would desire from us as service and how we could rely on your involvement. Bahaka Sunil Sabat is a Silicon Valley veteran of 11 years, who has worked and consulted with companies like Intel, I2, Cadence and Charles Schwab. Bahaka Subhranshu Das has a MCA and MBA to his credit with 15 years of total IT experience across the globe. He started his career with Govt. of Orissa and then moved to private sector in India, Japan, Canada, UK before finally landing in USA. At present he is working in USA for the last five years. The following is a schedule when Bahaka Sabat and Bahaka Das will be available for one-on-one discussions with you. On behalf of Nandighosha we welcome you to the meetings and hope we can provide you with satisfactory service after we receive your feedback. We look forward to your attendance in large numbers and hope to see you amongst us as a Bahaka.
