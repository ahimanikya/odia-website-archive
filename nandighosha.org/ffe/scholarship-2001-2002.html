In the next few days, the Foundation’s Mumbai office would be mailing these papers and the revised application forms to all active and registered facilitators in India.  I would appreciate it if you could carefully review the papers and ensure that your facilitators are thoroughly familiar with them before they begin the process of completing the application forms and submitting them to the India office.  Non-compliance with the requirements will not only delay the processing of the student's application, but it may also result in the rejection of an incorrect or incomplete application itself.

In order to ensure an effective relationship between coordinators, facilitators and students, it has been decided that a coordinator should have under him/her not more than 10 facilitators and that a facilitator should have under him/her not more than 20 students.  Accordingly, coordinators already exceeding the indicated levels are advised not to nominate any additional facilitators.  Facilitators with more than 20 students have been advised not to take on any more new, first time students. 

Coordinators who have fewer than 10 facilitators may nominate additional facilitators within the specified limit if they wish to do so.  To do so, the coordinator should complete and submit the enclosed facilitator enrollment form to the US office as soon as possible but not later than June 30, 2001 for approval,  registration and assignment of identification number.  Please make as many additional copies of the form as you may need.  We will not be processing applications received through unregistered facilitators.    

If you need any explanation on the policies or on the revised application forms, please contact Mr. V.K. Fondekar or me by phone or email.

Section A: Role of Facilitators

The facilitator disseminates information about FFE in friends and family circles, social service and community organizations, voluntary and non-profit organizations.

The facilitator reaches out to people with experience in the educational field and keeps abreast with the changes taking place in that area; he/she keeps FFE informed of such changes.

The facilitator seeks and identifies students eligible for FFE support.   A facilitator should not recommend any relative for the Foundation’s scholarship.

The facilitator gets to know the student well by meeting him/her several times before filling out the application.

The facilitator visits student’s family in order to assess and vouch for the student’s critical financial need.

The facilitator helps students fill out the application form and provides comments on the application based on his/her own insight and knowledge of student’s eligibility for financial aid.

The facilitator ensures that all the documents required in support of the application are enclosed with the application.  While doing so, the facilitator carefully scrutinizes the documents presented with the application and satisfies him/herself about their genuineness and completeness.

The facilitator disburses funds to the students and maintains proper records.  The facilitator hands over the cheque or draft to the student and receives the receipts for the payment.

The facilitator continuously interacts with his/her U.S. Coordinator while mentoring, monitoring and nurturing the progress of the student.

The facilitator scrutinizes expense statement submitted by the student, verifying the receipts in relation to the amount sanctioned, satisfies him/herself about the proper use of FFE funds before approving it.

Before recommending new students, the facilitator follows up on the progress of the students for whom scholarships were approved in the previous year.  Students who have completed their studies should be encouraged to keep in touch with the facilitator and the Foundation.  The students should be encouraged to send periodic reports to the Foundation on the progress in their careers and on the steps they have taken to fulfill their pledges made to the Foundation.

A facilitator is requested not to take a load of more than 20 students inclusive of new and renewal cases.

Should a facilitator be unable to continue to handle the responsibility for a current FFE scholar for any reason (travel, illness, or other commitments), he/she should make the necessary arrangements sufficiently in advance to ensure that the student’s interests are properly taken care of.  The facilitator should advise his/her coordinator of the inability to perform the expected roles and the period of time for which he would be unable to do so.  In such cases, the coordinator may, in consultation with the Foundation’s US office, locate another facilitator to take over the responsibility for the student.  The two facilitators should exchange all relevant information about the student and assist him/her in the application process.

The facilitator keeps in close touch with FFE, and follows instructions issued by FFE from time to time through his/her coordinator and through the India office.

While making his/her contribution, the facilitator always keeps in mind the mission, policies and practices of the Foundation.

Section B: School and college levels eligible for scholarship

All approved courses in recognized schools, colleges, universities and other educational institutions leading to: (a) school and higher secondary school leaving certificates; (b) degrees in arts, sciences, commerce, computer applications, and other courses; and (c) degrees and diplomas in engineering, medicine, technology, and other professional courses are eligible for the Foundation’s scholarship.

Vocational, technical and other courses with duration less than 3 years after school and/or high school are not eligible for scholarship.  Also courses in music, fine arts, and sports are not eligible.

Post-graduate courses in arts, commerce, computer applications and sciences are eligible.  However, post-graduate courses in engineering, technology and medicine are not eligible.

Students studying in the following school and college levels are eligible to apply for the Foundation’s scholarships in AY 2001-2002 provided they fulfill the eligibility criteria for academic standards and family income described in Sections C and D.  

1. School

Students in class/standard 8 to 10 are eligible.

2. High school, pre-university (PUC) or intermediate college 

Students studying in 11th standard or 1st year of PUC or Intermediate College are eligible. 

Current FFE scholars who were provided a scholarship for their studies in 11th standard or 1st year PUC or intermediate college in AY2000-2001 are eligible to apply for the renewal of scholarship for their 12th standard or 2nd year PUC or intermediate college studies during AY 2001-2002.

First time applicants studying for their 12th standard or 2nd year PUC or Intermediate College in AY 2001-2002 are not eligible for financial assistance.

3. Diploma/degree courses:

Students studying for their 1st year of degree/diploma courses are eligible.

Diploma students who have secured direct admission to the first or second year of a degree course are eligible.

Current FFE scholars who were provided a scholarship for their 1st or later years of degree/diploma studies in AY 2000-2001 are eligible to apply for the renewal of scholarship for their 2nd or later years of degree/diploma studies during AY 2001-2002.

First time applicants studying in the 2nd or later years of their degree/diploma studies in AY 2001-2002 are not eligible for financial assistance.

4. Exceptional cases

Students who had applied for scholarships during the academic year 2000-2001 but whose applications were rejected for one reason or the other, are eligible to reapply for the Foundation’s financial assistance, provided they satisfy the criteria for academic excellence and family income eligibility described in Sections C and D.

Section C: Criteria for assessing academic excellence

The following criteria will be used for assessing a student’s academic excellence.  If a student does not meet these standards, the facilitator is requested not to forward the student’s application to the Foundation.

1. Applicants in standard 8 to 10

Student should have obtained not less than 70% marks on the average for the previous 3 academic years; in addition, the student should not have obtained less than 60% marks in any of the three years.

If marks are less than 70% on the average but not less than 60% in any of the three preceding years, the student should demonstrate that he/she is in the top 10% of the class.

2. First time applicants and Current FFE scholars in standard 11 or 1st year PUC or Intermediate College

Student should have obtained not less than 75% marks in the 10th standard public examination conducted by the regional/state/central board of examinations.

3. Renewal applicants in standard 12 or 2nd year PUC or Intermediate College

Student should have passed the 11th standard or 1st year PUC or Intermediate College.

4. First time applicants and current FFE scholars in the first year of a three-year degree course

Student should have obtained not less than 75% marks in 12th standard public examination or final PUC or intermediate college examination.

5. Renewal applicants in second and third years of a three-year degree course

Student should have obtained not less than 60% marks and a first class in the first and/or second years of the degree course.

6. First time applicants and current FFE scholars in first year of a professional degree course in engineering, technology, and medicine 

Where admission is based on performance in a common entrance test (CET), the student should have obtained 75% marks or an overall rank not less than the top 20% of the students appearing and qualifying in the test.  In addition, the student should have obtained admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.  (For definition of “free” and “payment” seat, see item 17 below).

In cases, where no CET is administered and admission is based only on marks obtained in the 12th standard or equivalent examinations, the student should have obtained marks not less than 80% or an overall rank not less than the top 20% of the students passing the 12th standard or other qualifying examination and admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

7. Renewal applicants in second and later years of a professional degree course in engineering, technology, medicine

Student should have obtained marks not less than 55% in the previous academic year.

8. First time applicants and current FFE scholars in first year of professional courses other than engineering, technology, and medicine (Agriculture, Veterinary, Home Science, B.D.S., B.A.M.S., B.H.M.S., B. Pharm., B. Arch., Nursing, etc. whose duration is four years or longer)

Where admission is based on performance in a CET, the student should have obtained 70% marks or an overall rank not less than the top 25% of the students appearing and qualifying in the CET for admission in the respective course.  Alternatively, if the admission test was administered for two or more professional courses, the student must have obtained 75% marks or an overall rank not less than the top 20% of the students appearing and qualifying in the CET.  In addition, the student should have obtained admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

In cases, where no CET is administered and admission is based on a college administered test and/or on marks obtained in the 12th standard or equivalent examinations, the student should have obtained marks not less than 75% or rank not less than the top 25% of the students passing the examination and admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

10. Renewal applicants in second and later years of other professional courses stated above

Student should have obtained marks not less than 55%.

11. First time applicants and current scholars for studies at the first year of a Master’s degree 

Student should have obtained not less than 65% marks in the three-year university examination.  If marks less than 65 %, first class and rank not less than the top 10% of the students passing the degree examination.

12. Renewal applicants for studies in the second year of a Master’s degree  

Student should have obtained not less than 60 % marks in the first year of the degree course or rank in the top 10% of the class.

13. First time applicants and current FFE scholars for diploma courses

Where admission is based on performance in a CET, the student should have obtained 70% marks or an overall rank not less than the top 25% of the students appearing and qualifying in the CET.   In addition, the student should have obtained admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

In cases, where no CET is administered and admission is based only on marks obtained in the 10th or 12th standard or equivalent examinations, the student should have obtained marks not less than 75% or rank not less than the top 25% of the students passing the examination and admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

14. Renewal applicants in second and later years of a diploma courses 

Not less than 55% marks in the previous academic year. 

15. Diploma students (first time applicants and current FFE scholars) directly admitted to a degree course 

Where admission is based on performance in a CET, the student should have obtained 75% marks or an overall rank not less than the top 20% of the students appearing and qualifying in the CET.  In addition, the student should have obtained admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

In cases, where no CET is administered and admission is based only on marks obtained in a three-year diploma course, the student should have obtained marks not less than 80% in the examination or rank not less than the top 20% of the students passing the examination and admission on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.

16. Disqualification for scholarship

For all applicants, failure to pass an annual exam at the first attempt and in full will result in disqualification for scholarship eligibility.  In other words, passing on “allowed to keep terms” will result in disqualification for a scholarship.

17. Payment seats in engineering and medical professional courses 

Admission to engineering and medical professional courses is generally made on the basis of “free” or  “payment” or other category of seats.  The tuition and development fees charged for a “free’ and “payment” seat are considerably different.

In distinguishing between a “free” and a “payment” seat, the Foundation will be guided by the seat description in the allotment letter provided to the student by the concerned government bodies.  The tuition and development fees to be paid for a “free” or a “payment” seat are normally indicated in the CET application booklet.

As a matter of policy, the Foundation’s financial assistance will be provided only to students who are admitted on the basis of a “free” seat allotment.  Exceptions to the “free” seat only policy may be made for students who, despite having obtained an extraordinarily high score at the CET or other qualifying examination, have not been able to obtain admission on a “free” seat basis.  To determine  “extraordinarily high performance”, the student should have scored not less than 90% marks in the CET or other qualifying examination or an overall rank of not less than the top 10% of the students passing the CET or other qualifying examination.

17. Verification of Applicant’s Performance 

The student should provide copies of relevant marks and rank certificates duly attested by the school/college principal in support of his/her academic achievement.

If the student has appeared for a public examination (10th, 12th /PUC/Intermediate, CET), the student should also indicate on the application form, the hall ticket number assigned to him/her for appearance at the examination with an approximate number of the students passing or qualifying for the examination.

If the student has obtained admission to a professional course on the basis of a CET, the student should provide information on the marks obtained in the test in total or in percent and/or rank obtained with a statement on the number of students qualifying the examination.  The students should enclose letters inviting the student for counseling and/or the allotment letter showing the category of seat the student has been assigned.  Facilitators should confirm the marks, rank, allotment basis and other information on the number of students qualifying the examination, and the student’s relative performance amongst this group.

Where results are declared in the form of letter grades and/or a grade point average, the student should provide an explanatory statement on the details of the grading system and its equivalence to a marks or class based scoring system.

18. Exceptions

No exceptions to the above standards for assessment of academic excellence will be made for any reasons including illness of the student or for any other family circumstances.

Section D: Criteria for assessing income eligibility

The criteria for assessing the income eligibility will be based on gross family income.  Gross income is defined as income before deductions for taxes or for any other reasons.  Family refers to earning members in the household including father, mother, and unmarried brothers and sisters of the applicant staying in the same residence.  To be eligible for financial assistance, the family’s gross family income should not be more than Rs.7, 500.00 per month or Rs. 90,000 per year.  No exceptions will be made to this family income limit for any reasons such as size of family, number of children in school/college, location of family, cost of living or for any other reasons.

Students seeking financial assistance from the Foundation for the first time are required to submit an income declaration (pro forma statement A attached) or income affidavit (pro forma statement B attached) as described below.  Similarly, current FFE scholars advancing from one level of study to the next higher level, such as, 10th standard to 11th standard/1st PUC/Intermediate, or from 12th standard/2nd PUC/Intermediate to a degree/diploma course are required to submit an income declaration or income affidavit.  However, current FFE scholars in the same level of study, such as in school from 8th to 10th, in high school/PUC/Intermediate, or in a degree or diploma course are not required to submit the income declaration or affidavit.

The particular pro forma to be used would depend on the amount of financial assistance being sought by the student.  The declaration or affidavit should be completed on stamp paper of appropriate value.  

Students seeking financial assistance of less than Rs.10,000.00 are required to submit an income declaration as per forma statement A attached.  The declaration should be made in the presence of the facilitator and a witness.

Students seeking financial assistance in the amount of Rs.10, 000.00 or more should submit an income affidavit as per pro forma B attached.  The affidavit should be sworn before an oath commissioner, first class magistrate or a judicial or executive officer empowered to administer oath, or notary public

The income declaration or the income affidavit, as the case may be, should be specifically made for the purpose of applying for the Foundation’s financial assistance.  The declaration or the affidavit should be supported by any one of the following statements as applicable:

 1.                    For every employed member of the applicant’s family as defined above, pay slip or salary certificate from the employer showing the gross earnings.

2.                    For members of the family in self-employment or having business income, bank statement, income tax return, sales tax assessment or any other statement as may indicate the gross business or self-employment income.

Section E: Fees and other educational expenses eligible for financial assistance 

The Foundation’s financial assistance will cover, to a reasonable extent, tuition and other mandatory fees payable to the educational institution in which the student is enrolled.  The scholarship will also cover, to a reasonable extent, boarding and lodging expenses, transportation, books, school uniforms and such other expenses, if specifically justified by the student.  Caution, library, hostel or other deposits are not covered under the scholarship.    

Tuition and other mandatory fees will include development fees, examination fees, and library fees.  The details should be shown separately for each type of expense. 

Students are normally expected to stay with their families.  If stay away from the family is essential, the applicant should reside at a hostel providing free boarding and lodging facilities or in institutions where such expenses are met through charitable donations.

Transportation, if required, should be by least expensive mode of transport and using available student concessions.  If hostel expenses are covered by the Foundation’s financial assistance, then transportation will not be eligible and vice versa.

Book purchases by students will be covered only if prescribed by an educational institution and if the books are not available in the college/university or other institutional libraries.  Students should be encouraged to purchase used, instead of new, books or to share books among two or more students.

School uniforms will be financed only if required by the educational institution.

The Foundation may consider an overall sum or limit on the financial assistance to be provided.

A summary statement of estimated/actual expenses is attached to the application for financial assistance.  First time applicants must complete the requirements indicated in the statement for the current year.  Current FFE scholars must complete both the current and previous year particulars. 

All estimated expenses must be supported by a statement from the school or college authorities and attested by the facilitator.  A copy of the schedule of fees and other expenses payable as published in the student handbook or other similar publication should be attached to the application.  Receipts for payment must support all actual expenses already incurred.  The receipts must be clearly marked or numbered. 

Section F: Payment of Scholarship Amount

At the present time, the scholarship amounts are disbursed by checks or drafts in the name of the facilitator or in the joint names of the facilitator and the student.  Taking into the difficulties experienced by facilitators as a result of the current practice, beginning with AY 2001-2002, scholarship amount will be paid out only in the name of the student.  For this reason, it is advisable that each student opens a bank account in his/her name or in a joint name with the facilitator.  If the student is not eligible to open a bank account, such as when the student is a minor, the scholarship will be paid in the joint names of student and the parent/guardian.  Accordingly, the facilitator may advise the parent/guardian to open an account in the joint names of the parent/guardian and the minor child.  The bank account particulars should be provided in the application form.  It may be noted that the advice to open a Bank account does not carry with it any assurance that financial assistance will be approved; the sanction will be decided on the merits of the student’s application.  

Payment of the scholarship amount will be made by draft or cheque.  The India office will dispatch the scholarship cheque or draft made out in the name of the student, or in the names of the student and the facilitator, and/or the parent/guardian.  The office will send the cheque or draft to the facilitator for distribution.  The facilitator, in turn, will distribute the cheque/draft to the student and obtain the student’s acknowledgement for receipt of the scholarship amount.  At this time, the facilitator should also review the student’s progress and undertake such other actions as are indicated in the statement on roles of a facilitator.  This includes obtaining from the student receipts for expenses incurred from out of the Foundation’s financial assistance and any other document that may still be pending.  The facilitator is requested to ensure that receipts and all other required documents are submitted at the latest when the student next applies for renewal of the scholarship.  In the event the student is completing his/her course of studies, the facilitator should ensure that the student is reminded of his pledge to the Foundation.  The student should also be advised to inform the facilitator and the Foundation on his/her next steps following the completion of studies.

Section G: Guidelines to Facilitators for Completion of Applications

The facilitator is requested to observe the following guidelines for completion of the scholarship application form.  The guidelines are provided below separately for renewal applications and for first time applications.

Renewal applications

A list of the students nominated by the facilitator during the previous academic year is attached.

The facilitator is advised to review the progress of the current scholars indicated in the list and group them into: (i) those continuing at the same course level e.g., in school from 8th to 9th, 9th to 10th or in high school from 11th or equivalent to 12th or in college from 1st year to 2nd year and so on, and (ii) those moving from one course level to the next, e.g. from school to high school or pre-university or intermediate college, from high school or pre-university or intermediate college to a degree course and so on.

Current FFE scholars in the first group should be advised to complete the Application for Assistance (Renewal) form.  Current FFE scholars in the second group should use the form Application for Assistance (First Time).

If the facilitator is not sending an application on behalf of any of his/her current FFE scholars, he/she is requested to provide a statement on the reasons why an application is not being sent.  In case the student has completed his/her course of studies, the Facilitator may advise the Foundation about the student's activities since his/her graduation.  The student may be encouraged to write to the Foundation about his/her career plans, and how he/she intends to continue his/her association with and be of assistance to the Foundation in the future.

First time applications

If the facilitator is sending 20 or more renewal applications, he/she is advised not to submit applications for any new, first time applicant.

If the facilitator is sending less than 20 renewal applications, he/she can submit an application for financial assistance for a new, first time applicant.  However, the total number of applications submitted by the facilitator in the current academic year, including the first time applications, should not exceed 20 applications.

First time applicants should be advised to use the Application for Assistance (First Time) form. 

General Checklist 

The facilitator is requested to ensure that the students nominated meet the course, academic performance and income eligibility criteria outlined in these papers.

The student should use the correct form for submitting the application for financial assistance.

All the required documents in support of the bona fide status of the student and his/her individual academic performance should to be provided.  A responsible authority should have duly signed the original certificates.  The facilitator should compare the copy with the original and attest to their accuracy.

The facilitator is requested to ensure that the student has collected all the required marks and rank in class certificates, admit cards, in support of his/her academic performance.

If possible, the facilitator may collect announcements and other information published in the local newspapers or other sources on the regional/state/central/CET and university examination results.  The facilitator can use such information to make an assessment of a student’s relative performance in comparison with other students appearing and qualifying at the examination.  The facilitator is also requested to send the published information with the application or separately to the Foundation’s India office.  The Foundation will use this information in later years for the formulation of its policies for establishment of the standards of academic excellence.

The facilitator is requested to ensure that the student’s family income meets the Foundation's family income criteria.  The required income declaration or affidavit, as the case may be, should be attached with the application.

The facilitator may carefully review the student's requested amount for financial assistance from the Foundation.  The estimated expenses should be verified on the basis of information contained in student handbooks, brochures provided by his/her educational institution or as authorized by the relevant government authorities.  These statements should be attached as supporting documents to the student's scholarship application.

The student should be advised not to attach certificates for proficiency in sports, languages, and extracurricular activities.  Such achievements are not taken into account in assessing a student's qualification for a scholarship.  On the other hand, they unnecessarily add to the cost of making copies and higher postal charges.

The completed application with all specified attachments should be mailed to the India office as soon as possible but not later than the prescribed last mailing dates as follows:

Applications from students in school, high school, PUC, and

 Intermediate college: August 31, 2001

Applications from students in degree/diploma colleges: October 31, 2001

Applications from students in professional courses: November 30, 2001

APPLICATIONS SHOULD ONLY BE SENT TO THE INDIA OFFICE.  ANY APPLICATIONS SENT TO THE US OFFICE WILL NOT BE EVALUATED.

Applicants and facilitators are advised not to wait until the last date but to submit the applications as early as possible.  This will enable the smoother flow of applications and their processing.

The last dates indicated will be strictly enforced.  Exceptions will be made only when examinations are not held in time or there are delays in the declaration of results or for any other valid reasons. To enable such exceptions to be considered, the student should produce valid documentation to show that the delay was due to reasons beyond his/her control.

Upon receipt of an application, the India office will screen the application for completeness.  If an application is complete and correct in all respects, the application will be forwarded to the Foundation’s office for assessment of eligibility for financial assistance.

In case an application is incomplete, or is missing in some required information on the application form itself, or in the required supporting documents, or when a wrong form has been used, the India office will inform the facilitator of the deficiency in the application and set a time limit for provision of the missing information or for correction of the errors pointed out.  The office will also provide a reference number to be used by the facilitator and/or the student in further correspondence regarding the deficient application.   The facilitator and/or the student should remedy the deficiencies within the specified time limit and send the missing or corrected information or resubmit the application to the India office for further action. 

Section H: Communicating with the Foundation

The Foundation’s India office is located at the following address:

B Wing-G06, “Gurukrupa”

N.C. Kelkar Road

Dadar (W), Mumbai 400 028

Email:  ffe@im.eth.net

In all communications with the India office, the facilitator is requested to clearly provide the identification numbers and other particulars related to the facilitator and the concerned coordinator.  Students who are current FFE scholars should provide their assigned student identification number.  This will greatly assist the Foundation in the processing of the scholarship applications and addressing inquiries.

The India office will advise facilitators only if an application is incomplete or incorrect in any respect.  

Facilitators and students are requested to refrain from making inquiries on the status of their applications.  Similarly, US based coordinators are requested not to make inquiries about the status of the applications sent by their respective facilitators.  The Foundation will do its best to advise the coordinator and the facilitator  on the status of an application at the earliest possible time.

Each application will be judged on the basis of the Foundation’s policies and standards for eligibility.  The Foundation’s communication of its decision on any application shall be regarded as final.  No communication regarding a rejected application or for sanctions less than amount asked, should be initiated.  The Foundation will not be able to respond to such inquiries.
