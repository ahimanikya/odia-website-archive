Request to send Article for dUradRuSTi August Issue


Dear Friends,

            I believe that duradrusti is not only the mouth piece of oDiA samAja, but also it has established its own significance as an oDiA e-magazine. I have no doubt that duradrusti has created waves in oDiA literature world and in the heart of its readers. It’s been long since I lost my link with oDiA literature, but since past few years duradrusti has given me a new direction and I was able to cope with my source, I hope that might be true for some of you.

             In last issues our readers and writers had been very cooperative and supported us as required in all the time. This time it’s my turn as editor in chief to give duradrusti yet another magnificent look with all your cooperation. On behalf of duradrusti editorial board I would like to request you all to send articles for our next issue in the following address:

Ahimanikya Satapathy
6164 Newell CT
Fontana, CA 92336

Soft copies are preferable and you can send them to Ahimanikya@yahoo.com

         I will try my best and attempt to create a new link between you and oDiA literature world and I am banking on your support.

Thanks You
Ahimanikya Satapathy
