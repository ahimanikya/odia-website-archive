Scope and source notes

The search reviewed successful-URL Wayback indexes for duradrusti.org and the earlier odiya.org/duradrusti path, plus an exact query for the April 2009 PDF. Local review inventoried PDF and Word filenames across the source folders and 23 ZIP archives including nested archives. It did not read every unrelated document or inspect every historical capture. No additional published issue PDF or public Word download was recovered.

Temporary conversions used to inspect the Word files changed pagination and substituted legacy fonts. They are investigation files only, not restored issue PDFs. The original document bytes are preserved. No article, timeline or approved homepage text was changed.
