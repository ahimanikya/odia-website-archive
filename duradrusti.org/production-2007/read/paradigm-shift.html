Paradigm Shift of OdiSA

(Odisa Samaja + MyOdisa + Duradrusti)

- A journal report by Biswaranjan Jena, CE, OdiSA (2004-2006)

Unlike other organization that follows its traditional chronology, OdiSA has gone through a completely different paradigm in its journey from 2004 to 2006. During this paradigm shift, OdiSA has turned itself out from its confine into a free spirit & brought itself from inside out while keeping its integrity intact. And that is only possible due to its functional transparency as well as valuable contributions from prominent members to do something for good cause(s).The memoir of OdiSA defined below is a vivid description of its life’s journey filled with lots of experiences from 2004 to 2006.It has reached its destiny by crossing all the ups & down that came across its way. While its paradigm shift is not an easy journey as some might thinks it otherwise but at the end it is the transparency & honesty that made OdiSA stands apart unlike other organizations.

OdiSA starts its journey in 2004 with a fresh election for the board as well as electing the new CE keeping its eye on the future. After the fruitful election, the CE & other BOA members have agreed to take the responsibility to carry its future forward by holding their first BOARD meeting. Taking advantage of the internet age, BOA had a chat room conference which lasted more then 4 hours irrespective of individual BOA member’s time differences which indicates its first sign of dedication towards carrying it forward & to do something for orissa attitude. Even it was a lengthy one but the end result was very constructive & produced success full committees , out of which prominent of them are reconstruction of Duradrusti committee , Web mgt. committee , Finance committee & Public relation committee & so on. Organization chart hierarchy was given a formal approval as well. While ratification of OdiSA constitution took a while based on individual members feedback & suggestions. While the web mgt. committee performed its timely task of continual of domain registration, Public relation committee has put a tremendous effort in registering itself as a formal non-profit organization dedicated for oriyas around the world & for orissa. Meanwhile finance committee did not forget to file its yearly Income tax returns along with financial reports. Duradrusti committee has put its great efforts in reshaping its mouth piece “Duradrusti” in order to preserve Oriya culture, language & heritage, same time facilitates a platform to express individual oriyas views & news. The public relation committee with the help of BM-2005 committee had successfully hold its 2005 annual Bandhu-Milana in Toronto Canada in order to have a face-to-face interaction among members & discussed various issues w.r.t. to orissa & its developments. Also during this period OdiSA has successfully enhanced its free speech platform, known as “Myodisa” yahoo group into a new height. As a result not only it produced new truth seekers, leaders, entrepreneurs etc but also able to join hands with other prominent organizations those think with the same spirit & dream for a prosperous & developed orissa. The most prominent of them are Agamee Odisa, IIT Bombay alumini, Worldorissa & so on. The NIS/IISER issue has taken a center stage among all these organizations/groups which leads them to come under one umbrella & fight for the good cause of developing orissa. Many letters & emails , individual as well as group has been sent to PM of India, President of India, Educational ministry, Human resource ministry, Orissa CM etc. OdiSA provided it’s all possible unconditional support to Agamee Odisa & stands by it in every account that helps the movement forward. As a result currently the movement started producing fruits one by one. In year 2006, Public relation committee had put tremendous efforts in formalizing the Bandhu Milan 2006 at Los Angeles, California. Also the CE of OdiSA & President of OSA had come to a point of discussion of holding a joint get-together as well, even though it did not materialize but both organizations see it as a first stepping stone of forming a bridge for an open communication channel. In this great effort the CE of OdiSA is very thankful towards the BOA for showing its confidence on him in dealing the issue. Also during this period OdiSA has more then doubled the growth of its memberships & welcoming those members in open hands. Irrespective of all these important events in OdiSA’s changing life in 2004-2006, there are many more events which is very difficult for OdiSA to remember but it does not forget to show its gratitude towards many individuals both members as well as non-members , who have contributed one way or other in restructuring OdiSA. Out of which OdiSA would like to give its special thanks to members like Debasmita Misra, Satya Dash, Biswaranjan Pattnaik, Markandeswar Panda, Anil Panigrahi, Ahimanikya Satpathy, Bikash Panda, Amit ku. Misra , Chinmoy Nayak., Biswajit Misra , Biswajit Khandai , Dhirendra Kar , Pradyouta Sahu & many more. Non members includes Pravakar Satpathy , Chitta Baral , Lalit Pattnaik , Subhagya laxmi Mohapatra , Manoj Panda , Bijoy Misra , Asrujit Mohanty & many more those who have contributed immensely for Odia Samaja, MyOdiSA & Duradrusti’s growth by their actions behind the scenes.

Besides all these happenings OdiSA still in its development stages & seek its future leadership to continue with the same spirit as well as speed. Out of which, it would like to recommend few notes that might be helpful in its future growth. First & foremost ill be ratification of its constitutions to address various issues that came during this period. More open communication & a respect for the defined constitution of OdiSA. OdiSA will appreciate if individual members can be more constructive in nature & willing to take responsibilities to move forward. Maintaining the transparency & willing to sacrifice for the good cause of OdiSA as well as Orissa.
