About this restoration

This is a new index and reading interface built from the surviving files. Original homepage captures and reader pages remain available separately. Each PDF page has its own static HTML address, Previous/Next links and a thumbnail. Nothing is uploaded, and no scripts, forms or editing features are required.

PDF page images are labelled derivatives. Compact PDFs and local production variants remain separate downloads rather than duplicate issues. The Duradrusti logo is original reader artwork recovered from a July 2016 capture. This collection lists recovered material; it is not a claim that every published issue survives.
