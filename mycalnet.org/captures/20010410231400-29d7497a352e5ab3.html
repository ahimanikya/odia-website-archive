Calnet Information


Please do not use this information for unsolicited marketing.

A listserv operating in the similar way as ORNET is created on www.egroups.com. The list name is called CALNET. The postings would be pertaining to local events, local picnics, change of address/phone # etc for California Oriya residents.

To send your posting, please email to    calnet@egroups.com

IF YOU ARE A NEW MEMBER TO CALNET, PLEASE COME FORWARD TO INTRODUCE YOURSELF WITH YOUR NAME, BACKGROUND AND YOUR LINKS IN ORISSA. THAT WAY WE COME TO KNOW EACH OTHER. IF YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS CHANGES, PLEASE UNSUBSCRIBE TO CALNET FIRST FROM THE OLD ADDRESS BEFORE SUBSCRIBING FROM THE NEW ADDRESS.

Subscription Links and Email Command Addresses

ONElist makes it easy for people interested in our list to subscribe to it. It provide a special subscription URL that walks people through the subscription process for your list. The form of the URL is:

http://www.egroups.com/subscribe/calnet It also provide graphics that you can include on your web site. These graphics and the HTML code needed to display them are detailed on the Manage List screen for your list.

In addition, it provide several special email addresses:

calnet-owner@egroups.com  - Sends email to the list owners

calnet-subscribe@egroups.com - Subscribe to the list through email

calnet-unsubscribe@egroups.com - Unsubscribe from the list

calnet-normal@egroups.com - Switch your subscription to normal

calnet-digest@egroups.com - Switch your subscription to digest

For the subscribe, unsubscribe, normal, and digest commands, just send an empty email to the address.

Note: the calnet-owner@egroups.com address is just for people to contact for any problems/questions.
